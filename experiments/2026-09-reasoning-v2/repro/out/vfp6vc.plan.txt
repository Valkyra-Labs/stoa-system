This screen is a **Dashboard** designed for a school head to present term performance to the board. It provides a high-level, visual overview of key metrics without allowing edits, ensuring a static, professional presentation.

The layout features a **Header** with the term title and a "Print/Export" action. The main area begins with a **Section** containing three **Metric** cards (e.g., Enrollment Growth, Test Scores, Budget Health) to immediately establish current standing. Below this, a **Chart** section displays a line chart showing attendance trends over the term. Finally, a **DataTable** presents a concise list of top-performing schools or departments, allowing the presenter to quickly reference specific data points. The design prioritizes clarity and readability, using neutral colors and clear labels to facilitate an effective verbal presentation.
