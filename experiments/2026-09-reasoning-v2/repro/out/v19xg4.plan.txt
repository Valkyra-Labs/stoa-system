This screen is a decision-support tool for a financial adviser to compare two funds side-by-side before making a recommendation. It presents a clean, tabbed interface allowing the adviser to switch between detailed financial metrics and qualitative analysis.

The layout uses a main **Section** as the container. At the top, a **Header** displays the two selected funds and a **Button** to add a new comparison. Below, a **Tabs** component manages the view: the "Overview" tab shows a **Dashboard** with key **Metric** cards (e.g., expense ratio, yield) and a **Chart** visualizing historical performance. The "Deep Dive" tab features an **Inspector** pattern, displaying a **DataTable** of historical returns, a **Timeline** of major market events affecting the funds, and an **AuditLog** of analyst notes. A **Drawer** provides quick access to full fund descriptions and risk profiles. This structure ensures the adviser has all necessary data in one place without overwhelming them, guiding them to a confident recommendation.
