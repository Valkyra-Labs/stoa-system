This screen is shaped most by the rules around order and emphasis, working with lists, and evidence and actions. The primary focus is on the list of failed payouts, prioritizing the most critical or recent issues for immediate attention. Filters and bulk actions help manage and categorize the list efficiently. Each failed payout requires evidence for resolution, such as detailed transaction information and historical context, displayed alongside the action buttons. Confirmation steps are essential for any actions that could have significant consequences, ensuring the analyst understands the impact before proceeding.
