The screen is shaped by the rules of **order and emphasis**, **numbers**, and **states**. The finance director needs to quickly grasp key performance indicators (KPIs) and any anomalies before meetings. The most important values—like revenue, expenses, and profit—should be **emphasized upfront** and **right-aligned** for easy scanning. **Delta** and **Trend** components show changes against targets or previous periods, ensuring the director can judge performance at a glance. **StaleData** or **ErrorData** components are used to signal if the data is outdated or missing, ensuring **data freshness** is clear. The layout avoids **decorative regions** and **repeating numbers**, keeping the screen clean and focused on actionable insights.
