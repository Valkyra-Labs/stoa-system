The rules that shape this screen most are:  
- **Order and emphasis**: The most important information—the student's performance, behavior, and any concerns—must be front and center.  
- **Numbers**: Metrics like grades or attendance should show their basis (e.g., class average, previous term) and be right-aligned.  
- **Evidence and actions**: The teacher needs clear evidence (e.g., recent work samples, behavior logs) to support any decisions or recommendations.  
- **States**: The screen must handle partial or missing data (e.g., incomplete attendance records) with clear indicators.  
- **Working with lists**: If the teacher is reviewing multiple students, filters and bulk actions should persist to help them focus on the relevant student.  

These rules ensure the teacher can quickly grasp the student’s situation, back up observations with data, and prepare for the parent meeting effectively.
