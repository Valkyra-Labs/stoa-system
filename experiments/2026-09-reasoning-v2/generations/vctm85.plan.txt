The rules that shape this screen most are:  
- **Put the exceptions and the items needing action before the averages**: Failed payouts are exceptions and should be prioritized.  
- **Sort lists of changes by the size of the change, largest first**: Failed payouts with larger amounts or more critical issues should be addressed first.  
- **Show at most one level of emphasis per region**: The most critical failed payout should be clearly highlighted.  
- **Numbers must carry their basis**: Include details like the reason for failure and the amount to inform the analyst’s decision.  
- **State the consequence of an action before it runs**: When resolving a payout, the analyst must understand the impact of each action.  
These rules ensure the screen is focused, prioritized, and actionable for the analyst.
