The rules that shape this screen most are:  
- **Exceptions and action items first**: The portfolio manager needs to see what has changed most significantly since yesterday, so the largest deltas are shown first.  
- **Sort by change size**: The most impactful movements are prioritized for quick decision-making.  
- **Show units and time frames with values**: The manager needs to understand the context of each change (e.g., percentage vs. absolute value).  
- **Evidence and actions nearby**: Each change must be explained with reasons, sources, or linked to relevant data to support decisions.  
- **Stale data freshness**: If data is delayed, it must be clearly marked so the manager knows to act with caution.  

These rules ensure the screen is focused on actionable insights, prioritizing what matters most for the portfolio manager’s morning review.
