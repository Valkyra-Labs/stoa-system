The screen is designed to help a financial adviser compare two investment funds and recommend the better option to a client. The layout is split into two main sections, each dedicated to one of the funds. Each section includes key metrics such as performance, risk rating, and expense ratio. A central comparison area highlights differences and similarities between the funds. At the bottom, the adviser can select which fund to recommend and provide a brief rationale. The layout ensures all relevant information is visible at a glance, facilitating an informed decision.
