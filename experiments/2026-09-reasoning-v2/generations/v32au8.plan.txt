**Main Goal:** The screen provides the finance director with a quick, high-level overview of the company's weekly financial performance, enabling them to make informed decisions before meetings.

**Layout Support:** The layout is structured to prioritize key metrics at the top for immediate insight, with sections for trends, comparisons, and anomalies. A clean, minimal design ensures the director can scan data efficiently, while a sidebar offers quick access to detailed reports or filters. This supports the director's need for rapid, actionable information without overwhelming them with detail.
