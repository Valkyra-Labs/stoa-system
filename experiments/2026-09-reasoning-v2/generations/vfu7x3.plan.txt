The **ReviewQueue** pattern and **Evidence and actions** rules shape this screen most. The analyst must process failed payouts sequentially, requiring a clear **current position** indicator alongside **done** and **remaining** counts to track progress. The **Working with lists** rule dictates that filters persisting during the workflow (e.g., by customer or merchant) must be prominent via a **FilterBar**, not hidden. Crucially, the **Evidence and actions** rule demands that the reason for failure (the evidence) is displayed immediately next to the resolution action, not buried behind hover states. This ensures the analyst can make decisions with full context. Finally, the **Order and emphasis** rule requires sorting failures by impact (e.g., transaction value or retry count) so the most critical issues are addressed first, keeping the most important values obvious without reading every label.
