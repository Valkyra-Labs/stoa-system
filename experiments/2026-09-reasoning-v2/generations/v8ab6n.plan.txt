The primary rules shaping this screen are **Working with lists** and **Evidence and actions**. The analyst needs a persistent `FilterBar` to sort failures by severity or type, with `BulkActions` available for bulk rejections or status updates. Each failed payout must display `StaleData` freshness indicators and `AuditLog` entries to understand the failure cause before resolution.

The **Order and emphasis** rule dictates that failures requiring immediate human intervention (e.g., refund required) appear first, sorted by the magnitude of the loss or age of the failure. The `Inspector` pattern ensures that when an analyst drills into a specific failure, the evidence (error code, transaction ID, user context) is visible immediately beside the action buttons, preventing decisions based on incomplete data. Finally, **States** rule requires distinct UI for empty lists (no failures), partial data (processing), and error states (sync failures), ensuring the screen remains usable regardless of system status.
