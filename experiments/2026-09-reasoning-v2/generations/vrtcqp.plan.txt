The main goal of the screen is to provide the teacher with a quick and organized overview of a specific student to prepare for an upcoming meeting with the student's parent. The layout should prioritize essential information while keeping the design clean and easy to navigate. At the top, a header will display the student's name and the meeting details. Below the header, a primary section will contain key academic and behavioral metrics, such as grades, attendance, and recent assignments. A secondary section will include any relevant notes or communications between the teacher and parent. This layout ensures the teacher can efficiently review critical details and prepare talking points before the meeting.
