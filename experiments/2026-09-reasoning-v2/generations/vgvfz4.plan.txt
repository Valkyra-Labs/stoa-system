The main goal of the screen is to help the payments operations analyst efficiently review and resolve failed payouts. The layout should support this by clearly displaying each failed payout in a queue, providing all necessary details for analysis, and offering straightforward actions to resolve each case. The screen will use a review queue pattern, featuring a list of failed payouts as items to work through one by one. Each payout item will include key details like the amount, recipient, and reason for failure. The analyst can take actions to resolve the issue, such as retrying the payout or marking it as resolved. This structure ensures that the analyst can focus on one payout at a time, access all relevant information, and take immediate action to resolve each case.
