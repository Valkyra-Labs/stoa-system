The rule governing this screen most directly is **Evidence and actions**. The financial adviser must make a high-stakes recommendation based on a binary choice, so the decision-making process must be transparent. The two funds should be presented as a **Comparison** to ensure they are evaluated on identical metrics side-by-side. Crucially, the evidence for each recommendation (e.g., past performance, fees, risk) must be displayed immediately next to the recommendation, not hidden in a drill-down. The screen must also enforce **Order and emphasis**, placing the superior fund or the primary recommendation prominently at the top, while ensuring all numbers carry their basis (target vs. actual, or peer comparison) to support the rationale. Finally, **States** rules apply to ensure the screen gracefully handles missing data for either fund, preventing the adviser from making recommendations on incomplete information.
