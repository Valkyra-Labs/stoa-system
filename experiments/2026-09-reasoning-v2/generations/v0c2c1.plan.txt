The main goal of the screen is to help a financial adviser compare two investment funds and recommend the best one to a client. The layout supports this by placing a side-by-side comparison of the funds at the top, where key metrics like returns, risk, and fees are easily visible. Below this, detailed information about each fund is provided in tabs for more in-depth analysis. This structure ensures the adviser can quickly assess the funds and find detailed information as needed, facilitating an informed decision-making process.
