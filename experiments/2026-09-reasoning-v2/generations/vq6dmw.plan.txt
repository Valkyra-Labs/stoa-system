The **"Order and emphasis"** and **"Numbers"** rules shape this screen most. The head of school needs to clearly highlight the most important metrics first, such as student performance, staff retention, and budget usage, to emphasize key areas of success or concern. The screen must also show **numbers with their basis**, like comparing current performance to previous terms or targets, to provide context for the board. **StaleData** or **ErrorData** indicators are critical to show the freshness of data, ensuring the board knows if the information is up-to-date. **Comparison** and **Dashboard** patterns are ideal to group related metrics and provide a clear overview, avoiding clutter. The screen must be **static**, with no interactive elements, as the head of school will not change anything, so the focus is on **clarity and emphasis** for the board’s review.
