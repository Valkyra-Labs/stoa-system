The screen serves as a quick dashboard for the finance director to review the company's key financial metrics and trends at a glance each Monday morning. It includes an overview of financial performance metrics such as revenue, expenses, and profit margins, displayed with recent changes and trends. A chart section shows fluctuations in key financial indicators over the past week. Additionally, a datatable provides a detailed view of recent financial transactions, enabling the director to drill down into specific areas of interest. The layout consists of a header with the title "Weekly Finance Review," followed by a series of cards and sections for metrics, charts, and the datatable.
