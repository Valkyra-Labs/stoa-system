The main goal of the screen is to help the portfolio manager quickly identify and understand the significant movements in their investment portfolio since the previous day. The layout should support this by clearly distinguishing between the most impactful changes and providing detailed explanations for these movements.

The screen will start with a header that names the portfolio and highlights any urgent actions. Below this, a prominent section will display the top movers—assets that have seen the most significant price changes since yesterday. Each mover will be accompanied by a brief explanation of why it moved, such as recent news or economic events. Supporting this, a table will list all assets with their price changes and key metrics, allowing the manager to drill down into specifics if needed. Finally, a timeline of recent events will provide context for the day's movements. This layout ensures that the most critical information is immediately visible while offering deeper insights with minimal effort.
