The main goal of the screen is to provide the finance director with a quick, high-level overview of the company’s financial performance for the past week. This allows the director to identify any immediate concerns or trends before diving into more detailed analysis during meetings. The layout is designed to be concise and easy to scan, with key metrics prominently displayed at the top, followed by graphical representations of trends and recent significant financial events. This structure ensures that the director can grasp the essential financial status and recent changes efficiently.
