The **rules about working with lists** and **evidence and actions** shape this screen most. The list of failed payouts needs to be clear, with filters to narrow down the work, and the most critical items (e.g., high-value or urgent failures) shown first. The screen should keep context by showing the list and detail side by side, not replacing the list when viewing a payout. Filters persist to help the analyst refine their focus, and bulk actions are available for common tasks like retrying or contacting support. Each failed payout must show the reason for failure, history, and possible actions, with clear consequences for destructive actions like deleting a payout. This ensures the analyst can act decisively while maintaining context and understanding the impact of their decisions.
