The main goal of the screen is to provide a clear and concise overview of the school's performance for the board. The layout should prioritize key metrics and recent trends, ensuring that the head of school can quickly highlight successes and areas for improvement. A top-level header will name the school and the term, followed by a dashboard displaying key metrics such as enrollment numbers, student performance indicators, and financial health. Below this, a timeline of recent events will contextualize the metrics, and a chart will illustrate trends over time. This structure ensures that the most critical information is easily accessible and well-organized, supporting a focused and effective presentation.
