The screen is designed for a payments operations analyst to review and resolve failed payouts. It features a queue of failed payout items, each with essential details like the transaction ID, recipient, and reason for failure. The analyst can move through the queue one item at a time, reviewing each item's details and taking actions to resolve the issue, such as resubmitting the payment or marking it as resolved with a note. The layout consists of a sidebar with navigation, a main section for the queue of failed payouts, and a detailed view of the current item being reviewed, including actions to take.
