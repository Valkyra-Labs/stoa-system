The screen provides an overview of market movements since the previous day, helping the portfolio manager understand what has changed and why. It is organized into three main sections: a header with the title and time frame, a sidebar with filters and saved views, and a main content area. The main area includes a summary of key metrics (like total value and performance delta), a comparison of the portfolio's performance against benchmarks, and a timeline of significant events. Below this, a table lists all the assets with their changes, and a chart visualizes the performance trends. This layout allows the manager to quickly grasp the day's movements and dive into details as needed.
