The main goal of the screen is to provide the teacher with a comprehensive overview of a specific student to prepare for an upcoming parent-teacher meeting. The layout should clearly present the student's academic performance, behavior, and any notable events or trends. This includes a summary of recent grades, attendance records, and any feedback from other teachers or the student themselves. By organizing the information into distinct, labeled sections such as "Academic Performance," "Attendance," and "Teacher Notes," the teacher can easily review and highlight key points to discuss during the meeting. This structure ensures that the most critical information is readily accessible and promotes a well-informed and productive conversation.
