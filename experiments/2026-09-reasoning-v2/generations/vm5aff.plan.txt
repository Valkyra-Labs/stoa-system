**Main Goal:** Enable a teacher to efficiently gather, contextualize, and validate all relevant information about a single student before a parent meeting, ensuring a focused, empathetic, and data-driven conversation.

**Layout Strategy:** The screen prioritizes clarity and completeness. A prominent header identifies the student and meeting details. The core content is split into two main sections: a "Student Profile" card displaying key demographics and recent performance metrics (using `Metric` and `Status` components) for quick scanning, and a detailed "Meeting Briefing" section containing a timeline of recent events (`Timeline`), a table of specific concerns (`DataTable`), and a checklist of talking points. A sidebar provides navigation to related records if needed. This structure ensures the teacher has a holistic view without cognitive overload, separating high-level status from granular details, allowing them to prepare concise, evidence-based points for the discussion.
