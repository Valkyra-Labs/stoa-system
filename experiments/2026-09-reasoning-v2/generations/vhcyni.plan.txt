The **rules about evidence and actions**, **numbers**, and **working with lists** shape this screen most. The adviser needs clear, comparable metrics for each fund, showing their performance, fees, and risk, with deltas against benchmarks. Numbers are right-aligned, labeled with units and time frames, and freshness is indicated. The screen avoids decorative elements, focusing on the two funds side by side in a **Comparison** component, with all relevant data and evidence for each. The adviser must see the basis for each number, understand the change over time, and know the consequences of choosing one fund over the other. A **Button** to finalize the recommendation is placed clearly, with a confirmation step to prevent irreversible decisions.
