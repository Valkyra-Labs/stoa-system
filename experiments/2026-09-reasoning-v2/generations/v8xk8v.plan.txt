2. The shape of the work is setting two objects against each other to decide which is better. Only two funds are in play, and the decision is made on this screen.

4. The adviser decides which fund to recommend. They need to see performance trends, risk metrics, and fees alongside the fund’s historical data and how it compares to the other fund.  
5. The structure shows both funds side by side in a comparison view, with metrics and trends displayed clearly. A summary of key differences and a recommendation section appear at the end.
