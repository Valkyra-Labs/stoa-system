The main goal of the screen is to provide a portfolio manager with a quick overview of significant changes in their investment portfolio since the previous day, along with the reasons behind these changes. The layout is designed to highlight key performance indicators and recent events that impacted the portfolio's value. At the top, a header summarizes the portfolio's overall status. Below it, a card displays critical metrics such as total value, gains/losses, and top movers. A timeline follows, showing recent trades, market events, and other relevant activities. This layout ensures that the manager can swiftly grasp the most important changes and their causes, facilitating informed decision-making.
