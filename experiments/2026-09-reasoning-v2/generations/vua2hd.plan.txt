This screen is a decision aid for a financial adviser to compare two funds (e.g., "Growth Fund" vs. "Balanced Fund") and recommend one to a client. It uses a **Compare** pattern, displaying the two entities side-by-side across key financial metrics like expense ratios, expected returns, and risk levels.

The layout features a top **Header** identifying the client and the two funds. Below, a **Section** presents a **Comparison** table where rows are metrics and columns are the two funds, with visual indicators for performance deltas. A secondary **Section** includes a **Chart** (line or bar) visualizing historical returns for both funds over time. At the bottom, an **Inspector**-style detail area shows the selected fund's full description and a **Button** for the adviser to generate a personalized recommendation note or client alert. The design emphasizes clarity, allowing quick visual judgment to guide the final choice.
