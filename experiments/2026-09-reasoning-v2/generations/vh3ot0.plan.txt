The **Order and emphasis** rule shapes this screen most, requiring the presentation of the school's top performance metrics and critical exceptions first, with clear visual hierarchy so the board grasps the overall state instantly. The **Numbers** rule is equally critical; every metric must display its basis (e.g., vs. previous term or target) to provide context for the figures, avoiding ambiguity. The **States** rule ensures that if data is stale, partial, or erroneous, the screen clearly communicates this uncertainty rather than hiding it, maintaining trust. Finally, the **Avoid** rule prevents clutter by removing decorative regions and unnecessary navigation, ensuring the dashboard remains a focused, high-impact overview of the school's standing without requiring interaction, as the user will not change anything.
