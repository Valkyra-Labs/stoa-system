The **Working with lists** rule is paramount here. The analyst must maintain context while drilling in, so the screen uses an `Inspector` pattern where the primary failed payout is visible in depth alongside the list of remaining items, rather than opening a separate detail view. **Evidence and actions** are critical; the reason for failure and the history of attempts must appear next to the resolution button, not hidden behind hover states, ensuring the consequence of an action (e.g., "Resend" or "Flag") is clear before execution. **Order and emphasis** dictate that failed items needing immediate action (like duplicate attempts or system errors) are sorted by recency or severity to prioritize the most time-sensitive resolutions, while **States** requirements ensure the screen handles empty queues or stale data gracefully without breaking the workflow.
