For this preparation screen, the most influential rules are **Order and Emphasis** and **Evidence and Actions**. The screen should prioritize information that highlights the student's performance and behavior, emphasizing key metrics and recent trends that could be crucial for the discussion. Since the task involves preparing for a meeting, the screen must provide clear evidence and context for the teacher's observations and proposed actions. This includes showing relevant data, such as grades, attendance, and behavioral notes, alongside actionable insights and any necessary next steps, ensuring the teacher can confidently address the parent's concerns and discuss the student's progress effectively.
