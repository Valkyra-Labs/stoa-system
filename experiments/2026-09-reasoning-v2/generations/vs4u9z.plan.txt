The most influential rules for this screen are Order and Emphasis, Numbers, and Evidence and Actions. The financial adviser needs to quickly identify which fund is the better choice, so the most important information should be immediately apparent. This includes showing key metrics with their bases (e.g., previous performance or target benchmarks) to facilitate direct comparison. Additionally, each fund's performance data should be accompanied by relevant evidence, such as historical trends or recent news, to support the adviser's decision. Actions like recommending a fund should have clear, visible confirmations to ensure the adviser understands the implications of their choice.
