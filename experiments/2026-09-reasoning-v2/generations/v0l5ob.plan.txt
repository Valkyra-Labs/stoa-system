1. The analyst is a payments operations professional who checks failed payouts regularly. They open this screen to resolve issues quickly, often in response to system alerts or customer complaints.

2. The shape of the work is taking each item in turn to a decision, until none are left. The analyst reviews each failed payout individually to determine the cause and take corrective action.

3. The entities are failed payout records, with metrics like payout amount, error code, and timestamp. Each value is judged against a target (successful payout) and historical data. The analyst needs to see the error context, payout history, and resolution status.

4. The analyst decides whether to retry, investigate, or escalate a payout. They need to see the error context, payout history, and resolution status to make informed decisions. Incorrect decisions could lead to further failures or customer dissatisfaction.

5. The screen should start with a list of failed payouts, allowing the analyst to scan for anomalies. Each payout should have a detail view with error context and resolution options. A progress tracker or queue system would show how many payouts remain. The resolution actions should be prominently placed for quick decision-making.
