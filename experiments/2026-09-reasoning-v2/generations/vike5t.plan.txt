The screen provides an overview of what has moved in the portfolio since the previous day, helping the portfolio manager understand the reasons behind changes in positions and performance. It is organized into three main sections: a header with a title and actions, a sidebar with filters and saved views, and a main content area. The main area includes a comparison of today’s and yesterday’s positions, a list of top movers with deltas and reasons, and a timeline of key events. The layout is clean and focused on quick insights, with interactive elements for filtering and drilling down into details.
