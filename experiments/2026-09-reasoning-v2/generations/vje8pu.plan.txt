The main goal is to provide the teacher with a consolidated, at-a-glance view of the student's recent performance, behavior, and key concerns to facilitate a productive, evidence-based conversation with the parent.

The layout prioritizes clarity and hierarchy. A prominent header sets the context (Student Name and Date). The core "Student Snapshot" section immediately displays critical metrics like recent grades, attendance, and behavior status using clear cards and visual indicators. Below this, a "Recent Activities" timeline provides chronological context for specific incidents or successes. Finally, a dedicated "Preparation Notes" section allows the teacher to quickly jot down talking points or specific questions before the meeting. This vertical flow ensures the most critical data is seen first, while actionable preparation steps are easily accessible, reducing cognitive load during the pre-meeting review.
