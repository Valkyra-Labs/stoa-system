The main goal of the screen is to provide the teacher with all necessary information about a specific student to prepare for an upcoming meeting with the student's parent. The layout should clearly present key details such as the student's profile, recent academic performance, and any relevant behavioral or attendance issues. A structured format with distinct sections for different types of information ensures that the teacher can quickly find and review essential data. This includes a summary section for quick reference, a detailed section for academic performance, and a notes section for any additional comments or concerns. This organization helps the teacher stay focused and well-prepared for the meeting.
