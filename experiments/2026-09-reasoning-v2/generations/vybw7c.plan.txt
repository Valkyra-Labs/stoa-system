The screen is for a payments operations analyst to manage and resolve failed payouts. It displays a list of failed transactions, allowing the analyst to filter, sort, and view details. A sidebar provides navigation and filters, while the main area shows a table of failed payouts with key metrics like amount, status, and reason. Each row includes actions to retry, investigate, or escalate. A drawer allows detailed inspection of a selected payout. At the top, a header displays the page title and actions like "Retry All" or "Escalate." Below the table, a section shows summary metrics like total failed amount and number of failed payouts. The layout is clean, focused on resolving issues efficiently.
