The key rules shaping this screen are **Order and Emphasis** and **Numbers**. The portfolio manager needs to quickly identify the most significant changes in their portfolio since the previous day. Therefore, the screen should prioritize showing the largest changes first, emphasizing the most impactful movements. Each numerical value displayed should include its basis for comparison, such as the value from the previous day, to provide context for judgment. Additionally, the screen should highlight the most important values without requiring the user to read every label, ensuring that the most critical information is immediately apparent.
