The screen is shaped most by the **order and emphasis** and **evidence and actions** rules. It prioritizes **exceptions and items needing action** first, so the portfolio manager sees the biggest changes immediately. **Delta** and **Trend** components show the size of changes, sorted largest first, to highlight what needs attention. **Comparison** and **AuditLog** provide **evidence** for why things moved, showing the reason and history without requiring extra steps. **Status** indicators and **StaleData** help the manager know if the data is up-to-date. **BulkActions** allow quick responses to multiple changes. These rules ensure the screen is **actionable**, **clear**, and **context-rich**, helping the manager understand and react to market movements efficiently.
