**Main goal**: Help the teacher prepare for a meaningful, focused conversation with a parent about a student's progress, challenges, and next steps.

**Layout support**: The screen is structured to prioritize key information and actionable insights. A header sets the context (student name, date, and purpose). A sidebar provides quick access to student data, such as academic performance, behavior, and attendance. The main content area includes a summary of the student's strengths, areas for growth, and suggested talking points. A section for notes allows the teacher to plan discussion points and prepare for questions. This layout ensures the teacher can quickly access relevant data, organize thoughts, and prepare a clear, empathetic conversation.
