This screen is a morning dashboard for a portfolio manager to quickly assess overnight market moves and identify key drivers. It is laid out as a responsive page with a header containing the portfolio name, date, and navigation actions. The main content area features a "Market Summary" section with three large metric cards displaying total P&L, net inflows, and volatility, each with a delta indicator. Below this is a "Top Movers" section using a comparison component to show the top 5 winning and losing positions side-by-side, highlighting percentage changes and absolute values. Finally, a "Key Drivers" timeline section lists the top news headlines or macro events from the last 24 hours that correlate with the portfolio's performance, allowing the manager to understand the 'why' behind the numbers before diving deeper.
