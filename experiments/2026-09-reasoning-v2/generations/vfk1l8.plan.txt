The screen helps a teacher prepare for a parent-teacher meeting about a specific student. It is divided into three main sections: a **sidebar** listing all students, a **main area** with the student's details, and a **footer** with meeting preparation tools. The **main area** includes the student's profile, academic performance metrics, behavioral notes, and a **comparison** of the student's progress against peers. The **footer** has a **filter bar** for selecting meeting topics, a **saved views** section for quick access to common meeting notes, and a **button** to save or send the meeting summary. The layout ensures the teacher can quickly access relevant information and prepare efficiently.
