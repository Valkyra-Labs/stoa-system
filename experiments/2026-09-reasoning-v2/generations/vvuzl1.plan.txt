The main goal of the screen is to help a financial adviser make an informed decision about recommending one of two investment funds to a client. The layout supports this goal by presenting key information about each fund side-by-side for easy comparison. The screen features a header with the title "Fund Comparison" to clearly identify the purpose. Below the header, a "Comparison" component displays metrics such as returns, risk, and fees for both funds, allowing the adviser to quickly assess the strengths and weaknesses of each option. This layout ensures that all relevant data is visible at a glance, facilitating a thorough and efficient decision-making process.
