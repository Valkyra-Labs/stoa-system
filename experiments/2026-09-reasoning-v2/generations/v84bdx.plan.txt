The main goal is to enable a clear, side-by-side comparison of two funds to facilitate an informed recommendation. The layout prioritizes a high-level summary at the top, using a `Comparison` component to display shared metrics for immediate visual decision-making. Below this, an `Inspector` pattern allows the adviser to drill down into specific details, risk profiles, and historical performance for each fund. A `Status` indicator highlights the adviser's current recommendation, while a `Button` enables a quick save of the selection. By placing the most critical comparison data first and grouping detailed attributes logically, the screen reduces cognitive load and ensures the adviser focuses on the decisive factors without scrolling through unrelated clutter. The consistent use of headings and sections guides the eye naturally from overview to detail to action.
