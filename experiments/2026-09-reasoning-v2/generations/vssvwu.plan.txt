The screen helps the portfolio manager quickly assess what has changed in their portfolio since the previous day and understand the reasons behind those changes. It is organized into three main sections. At the top, a **Header** displays the title and a **FilterBar** to select timeframes or assets. In the middle, a **Dashboard** shows key metrics like total value, gains, and losses, alongside a **Chart** to visualize performance trends. Below, a **DataTable** lists all assets with their changes, and a **Timeline** or **AuditLog** explains the causes of significant movements. This layout enables the manager to grasp the overall status and dive into specific changes with ease.
