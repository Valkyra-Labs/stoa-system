This screen is a preparation dashboard for a teacher to consolidate all relevant information before a parent-teacher conference. It aims to present a balanced, evidence-based view of the student's performance, behavior, and social-emotional growth to facilitate an honest and productive dialogue.

The layout uses a primary navigation bar at the top for quick access to different categories (e.g., "Academic," "Behavior," "Notes"). Below this, the main content area is split into two columns: a left sidebar containing a concise summary of the student's key metrics and status, and a right pane dedicated to detailed records. The right pane features an interactive list of the student's recent incidents and achievements, with the ability to expand any item to show full context. A small panel at the bottom suggests potential talking points or preparation tasks. The design prioritizes clarity, allowing the teacher to quickly find positive highlights while having easy access to specific concerns if they arise during the meeting.
