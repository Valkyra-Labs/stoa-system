The most critical rules for this screen are "Order and emphasis" and "Evidence and actions." Given the teacher is preparing for a meeting with a parent about a specific student, the screen should prioritize information that highlights any issues or notable achievements of the student, ensuring these items are immediately apparent. The screen should avoid clutter and focus on the most relevant data for the upcoming discussion. Additionally, evidence and context for any actions or decisions should be readily available, such as recent grades, attendance records, and any interventions or support provided. This ensures the teacher can confidently discuss the student's progress and address any concerns with clear, well-documented information.
