The screen is designed for a teacher to prepare for a meeting with a parent about a specific student. It provides an overview of the student's academic performance, behavior, and any relevant notes or documents. The layout consists of a header with the student's name and meeting details, followed by several sections:

1. **Overview**: A brief summary of the student's key performance indicators and behavior trends.
2. **Performance**: A chart displaying the student's academic progress over time.
3. **Behavior**: A list of recent behavioral incidents, including dates and descriptions.
4. **Documents**: A tabbed section with tabs for Notes and Reports, containing any relevant documents or notes the teacher has prepared for the meeting.

This layout ensures the teacher has all necessary information at hand to discuss the student's progress comprehensively with the parent.
