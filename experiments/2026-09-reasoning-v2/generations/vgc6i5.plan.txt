This screen is a pre-meeting dashboard for a teacher to prepare a concise, empathetic summary of a student's performance and behavior for a parent call. It is structured as a `Page` with a `Header` containing the student's name and a "Start Meeting" action. The main content uses a `Section` divided into two columns: the left holds a `Card` with key `Metric`s (e.g., attendance, grades) and a small `Trend` visualization; the right holds an `Inspector` pattern where the teacher selects specific records (assignments, incidents) to see a detailed `Detail` view, `history`, and context notes. Below these, a `DataTable` lists recent events with `FilterBar` controls to narrow down behavior or academic issues. A `Status` badge indicates the meeting readiness state. The layout guides the teacher from high-level stats to specific evidence, ensuring they have a balanced narrative ready before speaking with the parent.
