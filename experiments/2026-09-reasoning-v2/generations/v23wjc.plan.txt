This screen is shaped primarily by the rules on **order and emphasis**, **working with lists**, and **evidence and actions**. The most important information for the analyst is the list of failed payouts that need immediate attention, so these items should be emphasized and prioritized at the top of the screen. Filters and bulk actions are essential for efficiently managing the list, allowing the analyst to quickly apply common resolutions to multiple payouts. Each failed payout requires detailed evidence and clear next steps, so the screen should provide all necessary information next to the action buttons to ensure informed decision-making and prevent mistakes. Confirmation steps are crucial for irreversible actions to protect against unintended consequences.
