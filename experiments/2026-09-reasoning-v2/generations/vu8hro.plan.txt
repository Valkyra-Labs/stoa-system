The **Inspector** pattern shapes this screen most, providing a focused view on a single student record rather than a broad dashboard. The **Evidence and actions** rule is paramount: the teacher must see the parent's perspective and the student's behavioral history immediately beside the proposed intervention, ensuring the rationale for the meeting is transparent. The **Order and emphasis** rule dictates that any pending parent communication requests or critical behavioral alerts appear first, sorted by urgency. Finally, the **States** rule requires distinct handling for incomplete records (PartialData) or outdated behavior logs (StaleData), as missing information directly impacts the quality of the preparation. The goal is a single, actionable truth about the student, with all context visible before the teacher leaves the screen.
