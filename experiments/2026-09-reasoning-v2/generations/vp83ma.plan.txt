The main goal of the screen is to help the financial adviser compare two funds and make an informed recommendation. The layout supports this by clearly presenting key metrics, performance trends, and risk profiles side by side for easy comparison. A header sets the context, while a section for each fund provides structured data, including historical performance, fees, and risk indicators. A comparison section highlights differences in key metrics, and a final recommendation section guides the adviser to choose based on the client’s goals. The clean, organized structure ensures clarity and supports decision-making by emphasizing the most relevant information.
