The rules that shape this screen most are **evidence and actions**, **numbers**, and **patterns**. The screen must present **clear, actionable evidence** to help the adviser make a recommendation, such as performance metrics, risk levels, and historical trends. **Numbers** must be presented with their basis (e.g., "10% return vs. 8% benchmark") and units to avoid confusion. The **Compare** pattern is used to side-by-side evaluate the two funds on shared metrics like returns, volatility, and fees. The adviser needs to see the most important data upfront, with the best-performing fund emphasized. The screen also includes **BulkActions** for selecting a fund and a **confirmation step** to prevent irreversible decisions. All data is shown in a **Comparison** region to ensure clarity and support informed decision-making.
