The most relevant rules for this screen are "Order and emphasis," "Numbers," and "Evidence and actions." The portfolio manager needs to quickly identify the most significant changes from the previous day to prioritize their attention. Therefore, the screen should emphasize the largest changes first, showing the deltas against yesterday's values. Each number should include its unit and the time frame (e.g., "1.2% change from yesterday"). Additionally, the screen should provide immediate context for each change, such as recent market events or specific transactions, to help the manager understand the reasons behind the movements. This ensures that the most critical information is immediately apparent and actionable.
