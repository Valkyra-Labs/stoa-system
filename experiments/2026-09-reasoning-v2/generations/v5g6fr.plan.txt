The rules that shape this screen most are: **order and emphasis**, **evidence and actions**, and **states**. The screen must prioritize the most important information — the student's performance and behavior — upfront, so the teacher can prepare effectively. Evidence like grades, behavior logs, and teacher comments are shown alongside key metrics (e.g., attendance, test scores) to support the teacher’s judgment. The screen also includes actions like "Add Note" or "Schedule Follow-Up" with clear consequences. Empty or partial data states (e.g., no recent attendance records) are explicitly shown to avoid surprises. The layout avoids decorative elements, keeps context while drilling into details, and ensures all data is fresh and actionable.
