This screen, called **ResolveQueue**, is a dedicated workspace for an analyst to investigate and fix failed payment transactions. It employs a **ReviewQueue** pattern, presenting one failed transaction at a time to ensure focused attention before moving to the next.

The layout features a **Header** with a clear title like "Failed Payouts" and a "New" button. The main area is split into two columns. The left column displays a concise **Card** containing the transaction's primary details (ID, merchant, amount, failure reason) and immediate **BulkActions** buttons (e.g., "Retry", "Escalate"). The right column acts as the **Inspector**, showing deeper context: a **Timeline** of the failure event, an **AuditLog** of internal actions, and a **Status** indicator. A **Drawer** overlays the screen to show the full merchant profile or transaction receipt when selected. At the bottom, a sticky **FilterBar** allows quick sorting by amount, date, or failure code, while **SavedViews** buttons let the analyst bookmark common filters for future sessions.
