For this task, the most influential rules are **Order and Emphasis** and **Evidence and Actions**. The financial adviser needs to quickly identify which fund performs better, so the most important metrics should be prominently displayed. Each fund's metrics should be side by side for easy comparison. Additionally, the screen should provide clear evidence and rationale for each decision, such as historical performance data and recent trends, to support the adviser's recommendation. Each actionable insight should come with a clear statement of its consequence, ensuring the adviser understands the implications of their choice.
