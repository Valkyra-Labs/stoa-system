**Main Goal**
The screen aims to empower the teacher with a consolidated, at-a-glance view of a specific student's recent performance, behavior, and needs, ready for a productive conversation. It transforms raw data into actionable insights, reducing preparation time and minimizing anxiety by ensuring nothing is forgotten.

**Layout Strategy**
The layout prioritizes clarity and hierarchy to support this goal:
1.  **Header & Context**: Clearly identifies the student and the upcoming meeting to set the scene immediately.
2.  **Primary Insights**: A high-level dashboard section displays key metrics (grades, attendance, behavior trends) using cards and charts for quick comprehension.
3.  **Detailed Evidence**: A secondary section offers a filtered, sortable table of specific incidents or observations for deeper discussion.
4.  **Action & Preparation**: A dedicated area lists actionable items or draft talking points to guide the conversation.

This structure moves from the "big picture" to specific details, ensuring the teacher enters the meeting confident and prepared.
