For this task, the rules that shape the screen most are Order and Emphasis, Numbers, and Evidence and Actions. The screen should emphasize the key differences between the two funds to help the adviser make a clear recommendation. Delta and Metric components will highlight important numerical values and their bases, such as returns relative to a benchmark. The screen should also provide evidence for each fund's performance, such as recent trends and key metrics, directly next to the recommendation buttons to ensure the adviser can see the rationale behind each choice. Additionally, since selecting a fund is a critical decision, each action should have a clear confirmation step to prevent mistakes.
