This screen is a Monday Morning Finance Dashboard designed for a Finance Director to quickly grasp the company's weekly financial health before meetings. It focuses on high-level trends, variance analysis, and key alerts rather than granular details.

The layout is a wide, single-column dashboard. At the top, a header displays the current week, fiscal period, and a "Download Report" action. Below, a top row of large `Metric` cards shows total revenue, expenses, and net profit, with `Delta` indicators for week-over-week changes. The central area features a `Chart` visualizing cash flow trends over the last 12 weeks. Beneath the chart, a `DataTable` presents a filtered list of top 20 transactions or departments, allowing quick drilling down. A `Status` banner at the bottom highlights any critical variances exceeding thresholds. The design prioritizes readability and speed, ensuring the director sees the "big picture" and immediate anomalies in under a minute.
