The main goal is to provide a teacher with a comprehensive, at-a-glance view of a specific student's profile to facilitate an effective parent meeting. The layout supports this by centering the student's identity at the top, followed by immediate status indicators and key metrics relevant to the meeting (e.g., attendance, behavior, academic progress). Supporting details like recent events, teacher notes, and communication history are grouped into distinct sections below, allowing the teacher to quickly scan for concerns or achievements. The design prioritizes clarity and hierarchy, ensuring critical information is prominent while secondary details remain accessible for deeper review if needed.
