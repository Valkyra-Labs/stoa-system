The rules that shape this screen most are **order and emphasis**, **numbers**, and **states**. The head of school needs to present a clear, focused overview of the school's performance, with the most important metrics (like student outcomes, staff performance, and budget usage) front and center. Numbers must be right-aligned and include their basis (e.g., targets, previous periods) to show progress. Stale or partial data must be clearly indicated so the board knows what's current. The screen should avoid decorative elements and ensure all data is presented in a way that supports quick, informed decision-making without overwhelming the viewer.
