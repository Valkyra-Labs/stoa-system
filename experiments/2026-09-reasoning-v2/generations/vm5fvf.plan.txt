The screen is a **Review Queue** for a payments operations analyst to resolve failed payouts. It displays a list of failed transactions, each with key details like payout ID, amount, status, and reason for failure. The layout follows the **ReviewQueue** pattern: a **toolbar** with filters and bulk actions, a **table** of failed payouts, and a **detail** view showing the selected payout’s full information. The analyst can filter by status, date, or reason, select multiple items, and apply actions like retry, refund, or escalate. The interface is designed for efficiency, allowing quick resolution of failed payouts while maintaining visibility into the payment process.
