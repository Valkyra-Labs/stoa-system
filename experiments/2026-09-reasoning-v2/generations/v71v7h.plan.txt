The main goal is to provide a clear, authoritative, and instantly scannable overview of the school's performance for a high-level audience with limited time. The layout prioritizes visual hierarchy by placing the most critical summary metrics at the top, allowing the board to grasp the overall health of the school within seconds.

The screen is structured to guide the viewer logically: starting with high-level key performance indicators (KPIs) to set the tone, followed by visual trend charts to explain the context behind those numbers. A secondary section provides a granular data table for specific deep dives, while a sidebar offers quick navigation to detailed reports or the audit log for transparency. This progression—from broad trends to specific evidence—ensures the presentation tells a cohesive story of progress, challenges, and achievements without requiring the presenter to explain basic navigation or layout.
