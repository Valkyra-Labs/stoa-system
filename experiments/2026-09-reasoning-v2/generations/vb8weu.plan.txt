The screen helps a financial adviser compare two funds and make a recommendation. It is divided into a header with the title and a brief explanation. A sidebar provides an overview of the two funds, including their risk and return profiles. The main content area features a comparison section that highlights key metrics like annual returns, risk levels, and expense ratios side by side. Below the comparison, a section shows the historical performance trends of both funds using line charts. Finally, a card at the bottom summarizes the adviser’s recommendation based on the data, with a button to finalize the choice. The layout ensures clarity, data-driven insights, and a clear recommendation.
