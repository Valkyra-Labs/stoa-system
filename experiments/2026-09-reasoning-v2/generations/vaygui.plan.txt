The screen is for resolving failed payouts, allowing the analyst to review, filter, and resolve payment issues. It is divided into a sidebar with navigation and filters, a main area with a list of failed payouts, and a detail view for each selected payout. The main area includes a table with columns like payout ID, amount, reason for failure, and status. A toolbar at the top includes filters for date ranges, payment methods, and failure reasons. Each row can be clicked to open a detail panel showing more information and resolution options. The layout ensures the analyst can efficiently work through the list while having access to relevant details and actions.
