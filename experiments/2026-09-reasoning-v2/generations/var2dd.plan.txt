The **Inspector** pattern shapes this screen most, as the teacher needs to examine one specific student in depth rather than browse a broad list. This necessitates a layout with a **primary** view of the student, a **context** panel for related metrics (grades, behavior), and a **history** log of events.

The **Evidence and actions** rule is critical: the teacher must see the rationale for every decision (e.g., "why" a grade was lowered) immediately next to the decision, avoiding hidden details. The **Order and emphasis** rule dictates that exceptions (behavioral alerts, failing grades) appear before averages, ensuring the parent meeting preparation focuses on issues requiring discussion. Finally, the **States** rule requires designing empty or partial states for data regions (e.g., if a teacher has no records for a student), ensuring the screen remains functional even with incomplete data.
