The rules that shape this screen most are: **"Put the exceptions and the items needing action before the averages"**, **"Show at most one level of emphasis per region"**, and **"State the consequence of an action before it runs"**. These ensure the teacher sees the student's most critical issues first, without being overwhelmed by less urgent data. The screen should highlight the student's current performance, any pending actions, and the parent meeting details prominently. It should also show the consequences of any decisions the teacher might make during the meeting, such as interventions or support plans. The layout should guide the teacher through the student’s profile, key concerns, and next steps, keeping the focus on the most important information for the upcoming meeting.
