The screen is a dashboard for the finance director to quickly review the company’s weekly financial performance before Monday meetings. It is laid out with a header showing the week’s date and a summary title. On the left, a sidebar provides quick links to key financial areas like cash flow, expenses, and forecasts. The main area features a grid of metrics such as revenue, profit, and cost trends, with color-coded deltas to show changes from the previous week. Below, a line chart displays weekly revenue trends, and a table lists the top expenses. A filter bar allows the director to select specific departments or time frames. The layout is clean, focused, and designed for rapid scanning of key financial insights.
