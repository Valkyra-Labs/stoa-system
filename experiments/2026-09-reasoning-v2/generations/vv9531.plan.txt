The main goal of the screen is to provide the teacher with a comprehensive overview of a specific student to prepare for an upcoming meeting with the student's parent. The layout should be organized to highlight key information and facilitate quick reference. A header will name the student and indicate the parent meeting. The primary content will be a summary section with essential details like the student’s name, grade, and contact information. Below this, a timeline of recent events involving the student will help the teacher recall important interactions and achievements. Finally, a section for notes will allow the teacher to jot down reminders and talking points for the meeting. This layout ensures that the most relevant information is easily accessible, helping the teacher feel prepared and confident during the discussion.
