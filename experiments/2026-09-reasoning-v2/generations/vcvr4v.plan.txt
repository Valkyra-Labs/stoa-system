The rules that shape this screen most are **evidence and actions**, **order and emphasis**, and **numbers**. The screen must clearly present the **evidence** for each fund (e.g., historical performance, risk, fees) so the adviser can make an informed recommendation. **Order and emphasis** ensure the most critical metrics (like returns, risk, and fees) are highlighted upfront, with the best-performing fund emphasized. **Numbers** are presented with their **basis** (e.g., "12% return vs. 8% benchmark") and **units** to ensure clarity. The adviser needs to **compare** the funds directly, so a **Comparison** component is ideal, with **Delta** and **Trend** components showing performance against benchmarks. The **actions** (e.g., recommend) are clearly labeled with their **consequences**, ensuring the adviser understands the impact of their decision.
