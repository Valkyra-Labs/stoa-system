The main goal is to deliver a clear, authoritative, and trustworthy overview of the school's performance for the board presentation. The layout prioritizes high-level metrics at the top to immediately answer "how is the school doing," followed by visual trends to show direction and context.

The structure uses a fixed header for the title and presentation controls, ensuring the screen looks intentional on any device. A primary dashboard section displays key metrics side-by-side for quick comparison, while a secondary chart area illustrates growth or decline trends. Below this, a filtered data table allows the presenter to drill into specific areas like academics, finance, or student life without clutter. The design avoids complex interactivity; everything is static and pre-baked for smooth, distraction-free projection. Spacing and contrast are optimized for readability from a distance, ensuring the narrative of the school's success is the undeniable focal point.
