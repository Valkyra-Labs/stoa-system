The rules that shape this screen most are **order and emphasis**, **numbers**, and **states**. The finance director needs to quickly grasp key performance indicators (KPIs) and anomalies, so the most important numbers—like revenue, expenses, and profit—are emphasized upfront, right-aligned, and clearly labeled with their basis (e.g., previous week or target). The screen prioritizes exceptions and action items, placing them above averages to highlight what needs attention. **StaleData** or **ErrorData** states are shown if the data is outdated or failed to load, ensuring the director knows the reliability of the numbers. The layout avoids decorative elements and repetitive data, keeping it clean and focused on actionable insights.
