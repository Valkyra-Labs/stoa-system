The main goal of the screen is to provide a clear and comprehensive overview of the school's performance for the board, without requiring any interaction. The layout should prioritize readability and ease of understanding, presenting key metrics and trends in a structured manner. A header will introduce the topic, followed by a dashboard displaying essential metrics such as student enrollment, attendance rates, and academic performance. Below this, charts will illustrate trends over time, and a data table will offer detailed insights. This layout ensures that the board can quickly grasp the school's current state and recent developments.
