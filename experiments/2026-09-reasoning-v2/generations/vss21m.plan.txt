The main goal of the screen is to help the payments operations analyst efficiently review and resolve a list of failed payouts. The layout is designed to streamline this process by presenting each failed payout in a clear, concise manner, along with necessary details and resolution options. The screen features a list of failed payouts at the top, with each item expandable to reveal more details and actions. This ensures that the analyst can quickly scan the list and focus on the specific payout needing attention. Below the list, a section for resolving the selected payout provides all necessary controls and information in one place, minimizing the need to navigate away from the primary task. This layout keeps the workflow intuitive and reduces cognitive load, enabling the analyst to resolve issues swiftly and accurately.
