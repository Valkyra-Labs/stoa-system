**Main goal**: Help the teacher prepare for a meaningful and focused conversation with a parent about a student's progress, behavior, and needs. The layout should prioritize key information about the student, relevant observations, and actionable insights, while keeping the teacher's preparation task clear and structured.

**Layout support**: The screen is organized into sections that guide the teacher through preparation steps: student details, academic progress, behavior notes, and suggested discussion points. A sidebar provides quick access to relevant student data, while the main content area is clean and focused, allowing the teacher to review and refine their notes efficiently before the meeting. The layout ensures clarity, reduces cognitive load, and supports a productive, well-prepared conversation.
