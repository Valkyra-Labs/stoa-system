The screen provides an overview of what has changed in the portfolio since the previous day, helping the portfolio manager understand the impact of market movements and internal decisions. It is divided into three main sections: a header with a title and summary metrics, a main area with a table of assets showing changes, and a sidebar with filters and a timeline of recent events. The table highlights assets with significant changes, showing their current value, delta, and reasons for the change. The timeline shows key events like trades or market updates. The layout ensures the manager can quickly scan for anomalies and drill into details.
