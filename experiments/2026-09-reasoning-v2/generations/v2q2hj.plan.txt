The rules that shape this screen most are the **evidence and actions** and **order and emphasis** rules. The adviser needs clear, side-by-side comparison of the two funds, showing their performance, risk, and fees, with the most important metrics (like return, risk, and fees) emphasized upfront. The **comparison** pattern is ideal here, placing the funds side by side on shared metrics. Evidence like historical performance, risk metrics, and fees must be shown alongside each fund, not hidden. The **order and emphasis** rule ensures that the most critical information (like returns and risk) is presented first, so the adviser can make an informed recommendation. The screen must also include **actions** like "Recommend Fund A" or "Recommend Fund B," clearly showing the consequence of each choice.
