The rules that shape this screen most are:

1. **Order and emphasis**: Exceptions and items needing action are shown first to highlight what moved since yesterday.
2. **Numbers**: Every number shows its basis (like yesterday’s value) and is right-aligned for clarity.
3. **Working with lists**: Filters persist to maintain context while drilling into changes.
4. **Evidence and actions**: Reasons for changes are shown next to the data, not hidden, to support decision-making.
5. **States**: Empty or stale data states are explicitly shown if the data is missing or outdated.

These rules ensure the portfolio manager sees the most important changes first, understands their context, and has all the evidence needed to act.
