1. The finance director opens this screen weekly to review financial data before meetings. It's a routine check to stay informed and prepared.  

2. The shape of the work is getting a general picture of many unrelated numbers with no action expected on this screen.  

3. The entities are financial metrics (revenue, expenses, profit) across departments and time frames. Values are judged against targets and previous weeks.  

4. The director decides if performance is on track. They need to see context like trends, variances, and data freshness to make informed decisions.  

5. The structure starts with an overview of key metrics, followed by departmental breakdowns and a comparison to targets. A timeline shows weekly trends, and a drawer provides detailed data sources and audit logs.
