The screen helps a financial adviser compare two funds and recommend one to a client. It is divided into a header with the task title, a sidebar with navigation, and a main area with two fund cards. Each card displays key metrics like returns, risk, fees, and performance trends. A comparison section shows side-by-side metrics for both funds. A decision summary at the bottom asks the adviser to choose one fund, with a recommendation button. The layout guides the adviser through data, analysis, and final decision-making.
