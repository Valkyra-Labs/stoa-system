This screen, titled "Student Conference Prep," is a focused workspace for a teacher to organize all relevant information before meeting a parent. It uses a layout with a sidebar for quick navigation between student profiles, and a main area divided into three sections: a high-level dashboard of recent metrics (attendance, behavior, grades), a detailed timeline of specific incidents or achievements, and a comparison view showing this student's progress against class averages or previous terms.

The interface features a "Student Profile" header with key actions like "Edit Notes" or "Export Report." Below, a summary section displays critical metrics such as engagement levels and behavioral trends. A central timeline highlights specific events leading up to the conference, allowing the teacher to prepare a narrative. Finally, a comparison chart visually places the student's data against peer groups to provide context. The design prioritizes clarity, ensuring the teacher can quickly identify strengths and areas for discussion to have a productive conversation.
