**1. Person and Session:** The finance director checks the screen weekly on Mondays to get a quick overview of the company's financial performance before meetings. No specific trigger other than the routine weekly review.

**2. Shape of Work:** Getting a general picture of many related numbers, with no immediate action required on this screen. The numbers are high-level indicators across various departments.

**3. Information:** Key financial metrics (revenue, expenses, profit) across different departments for the current week compared to the previous week and targets. Time frame is the current week.

**4. Decision and Evidence:** No specific decision is required here; the purpose is to inform the finance director about the overall financial health. Evidence needed includes variance from targets and previous week's numbers.

**5. Structure:** 
- **Header:** Title and subtitle for the screen.
- **Main Section:** High-level financial metrics for each department.
- **Comparison Section:** Metrics compared to previous week and targets.
- **Footer:** Optional summary or next steps.

This structure ensures the finance director can quickly grasp the financial performance across departments and understand variances.
