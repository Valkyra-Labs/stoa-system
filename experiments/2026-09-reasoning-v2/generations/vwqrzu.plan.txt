**Main goal**: Help the portfolio manager quickly identify which assets have changed in value since yesterday and understand the reasons behind those changes, enabling informed decision-making.

**Layout support**: The screen is structured to prioritize clarity and focus. A header at the top provides context and a summary of the time frame. A **comparison** section shows assets side-by-side with their **metrics** and **delta** changes. A **timeline** or **audit log** explains the reasons for changes. A **filter bar** allows refining the view by asset type, date, or performance. A **drawer** or **sidebar** offers additional details like news, reports, or analyst comments. This layout ensures the manager can scan key data first, dive into details when needed, and maintain a clear overview of portfolio movements.
