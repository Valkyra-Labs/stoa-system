The main goal is to enable a payments operations analyst to efficiently identify, investigate, and resolve failed payouts with minimal friction. The layout prioritizes a high-density, scannable data table at the top, allowing rapid filtering and sorting of failure reasons.

Below the table, a dedicated "Resolve" section provides immediate access to common fixes (e.g., retry, refund, manual override) via quick-action buttons, reducing navigation steps. To the right, a persistent sidebar offers contextual details for the currently selected payout, preventing the need to switch views for every action. This structure separates broad list management from specific record inspection, keeping the primary workflow—scanning and fixing—streamlined while ensuring critical context remains always visible.
