The main goal of the screen is to help a payments operations analyst review and resolve a list of failed payouts efficiently. The layout is designed to guide the analyst through each payout one at a time, providing all necessary details and actions upfront to minimize context switching. The screen consists of a header that names the task, a primary area for the current payout item with its details and resolution options, and a secondary area showing the list of remaining items to process. This setup ensures the analyst can focus on one task at a time while being aware of their progress through the list.
