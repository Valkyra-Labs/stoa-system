The main goal of the screen is to provide the finance director with a quick overview of the company's key financial metrics for the past week, enabling them to prepare for meetings efficiently. The layout is designed to prioritize clarity and speed of comprehension, with the most critical information, such as revenue, expenses, and profit, displayed prominently at the top. Below this, secondary metrics like cash flow and key performance indicators (KPIs) are organized into distinct, labeled sections for easy reference. This structure ensures that the finance director can swiftly grasp the essential financial health and trends of the company.
