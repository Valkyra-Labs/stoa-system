The **order and emphasis** and **evidence and actions** rules shape this screen most. The head of school needs to present a clear, focused overview of the school’s performance, with the most important metrics (like student outcomes, staff performance, and financial health) emphasized upfront. The screen should avoid clutter and present data in a way that highlights key achievements and areas needing attention. 

The **evidence and actions** rule ensures that all data is backed by context—such as trends, comparisons, and sources—so the board can understand the basis for each metric. Since the screen is static and not meant to be changed, it should also clearly show the status of data (e.g., stale or partial) and avoid misleading representations. The layout must be clean, with clear sections for different domains of performance.
