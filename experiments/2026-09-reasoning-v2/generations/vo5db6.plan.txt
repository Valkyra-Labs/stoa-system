The rules shaping this screen most are **Order and emphasis** (prioritizing exceptions and sorting changes by magnitude) and **Numbers** (ensuring every metric shows its basis like target vs. actual). The finance director needs immediate judgment calls, so exceptions and variance-driven items must appear first, followed by stable averages. **Working with lists** dictates that filters persist and bulk actions are offered for routine adjustments, while **Evidence and actions** require decisions to be immediately supported by context (history or source) without extra clicks. Finally, **States** ensures the dashboard remains usable even if data is stale or missing, preventing panic during a Monday review. The goal is a single view where the most critical deviations pop out instantly, allowing the director to act on the largest variances before meeting.
