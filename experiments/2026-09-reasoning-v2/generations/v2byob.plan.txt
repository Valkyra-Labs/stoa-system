The rules shaping this screen are **Order and emphasis** (prioritizing exceptions and sorting by change magnitude), **Numbers** (ensuring every metric includes its basis like target or prior period), and **Evidence and actions** (displaying reasons and history directly alongside metrics).

The Finance Director needs immediate, actionable intelligence, not decorative KPIs. By placing the largest variances and exceptions at the top, the screen allows for rapid triage. Every number shown must explicitly state its basis (e.g., "vs. last week" or "vs. budget") to enable instant judgment. Since decisions often require context, the design avoids hiding evidence; reasons for variances and source data are visible inline. Finally, because these are high-stakes weekly reviews, the screen must handle empty, stale, or error states gracefully, ensuring the user knows if data is missing or outdated before the meeting starts.
