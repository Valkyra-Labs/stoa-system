The main goal of the screen is to help a payments operations analyst resolve failed payouts efficiently. The layout should guide the analyst through each failed payout, providing all necessary details and actions in a clear, organized manner. At the top, a header identifies the task as "Resolve Failed Payouts." Below it, a list of pending payouts is displayed, with each item showing key details like the payout ID, recipient, and reason for failure. Clicking on an item reveals additional details and a set of actions to resolve the issue, such as retrying the payout, updating the recipient's information, or marking it as resolved. This structure ensures the analyst can focus on one payout at a time, reducing cognitive load and minimizing errors.
