The main goal is to enable a financial adviser to make a confident, data-driven recommendation between two funds by presenting a clear, side-by-side comparison that highlights key differentiators. The layout prioritizes direct comparison to reduce cognitive load.

A top header identifies the specific client and the two funds being evaluated. Below this, a central comparison card displays shared metrics (like expense ratio and risk) and unique features for each fund in parallel columns. A dedicated "Decision Factors" section highlights the top three reasons for the recommendation, ensuring the rationale is immediately visible. Finally, a prominent "Recommendation" button provides a single, low-friction action to finalize the choice. This structure guides the adviser's eye from objective data to the final decision, minimizing distractions and ensuring all critical information is accessible in one glance.
