1. The portfolio manager opens the screen daily to review changes in their portfolio, checking for unusual movements and understanding their causes.  
2. The shape of the work is scanning a large collection for deviations, with decisions made elsewhere.  
3. The entities are portfolio holdings, measures include price changes and performance, judged against yesterday's values. Key values are price deltas and reasons for changes.  
4. The manager decides whether to investigate or act on a change. They need to see the source, freshness, and context of each change.  
5. The structure starts with a summary of changes, followed by a list of affected holdings, with details and reasons for each change. The action buttons are at the end for further steps.
