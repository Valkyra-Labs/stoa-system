The main goal of the screen is to present a clear, concise overview of the school's performance for the board, highlighting key metrics and trends without requiring any interaction. The layout should be organized to draw attention to the most important information at a glance, ensuring the head of school can confidently present the data. A top-level header will identify the school and the term being reviewed. Below this, a series of cards or sections will display critical metrics such as student attendance, academic performance, and financial health. Each card will include a title, a key metric, and a trend indicator to show recent changes. This layout ensures that the data is easily digestible and emphasizes the school’s overall status and performance trends.
