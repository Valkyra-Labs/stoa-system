1. The analyst is a payments operations professional who regularly reviews payout failures to resolve issues. They open this screen to address a batch of failed transactions, likely triggered by system alerts or manual checks.

2. The shape of the work is taking each item in turn to a decision, until none are left. The analyst reviews each failed payout, investigates the cause, and takes action to resolve it, often requiring interaction with other systems or teams.

3. The entities are failed payout records, with measures like payout status, error code, and timestamp. Each value is judged against a target (successful payout) and historical data. The analyst needs to see the error context, payout details, and resolution history.

4. The decision is whether the payout can be retried, escalated, or marked as resolved. The analyst needs to see the error context, payout details, and resolution history to decide effectively. A wrong decision could lead to repeated failed attempts or customer dissatisfaction.

5. The structure starts with a list of failed payouts, each with a card showing key details. The analyst selects one to view in-depth, with contextual information and resolution options. Progress through the work is shown by marking payouts as resolved. Actions and their consequences are displayed at the end of each card.
