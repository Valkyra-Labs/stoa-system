The main goal of the screen is to provide a portfolio manager with a quick overview of the significant movements in their investment portfolio since the previous day, along with the reasons behind these changes. The layout should support this goal by presenting the most crucial information at a glance, followed by detailed insights. The screen will feature a header that clearly identifies the page, a central section for high-impact movements, and a sidebar for filtering and additional context. This arrangement ensures that the manager can swiftly assess the day's critical shifts and delve into the underlying factors affecting their portfolio.
