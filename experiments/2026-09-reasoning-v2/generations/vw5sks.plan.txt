The primary rules shaping this screen are **Order and emphasis** and **Numbers**. The Finance Director needs to identify anomalies immediately, so exceptions and items requiring action must appear before averages, sorted by the magnitude of the change. Every metric displayed must explicitly state its basis (e.g., target vs. actual, week-over-week) to support rapid judgment without reading legends. Additionally, **Evidence and actions** are critical; any flagged variance must show the source data and historical context inline, not hidden in hover states, to enable quick verification before the meeting.

Secondary rules include **States** (handling stale or partial data gracefully) and **Avoid** (preventing decorative KPI cards that don't drive action). The layout will focus on a high-density table of deviations rather than isolated charts, ensuring the screen serves as a decision tool rather than a visual display.
