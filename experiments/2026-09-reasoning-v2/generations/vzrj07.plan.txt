**Main Goal:** The screen helps a financial adviser compare two funds and make an informed recommendation based on key metrics, risk, returns, and client goals.

**Layout Support:** The layout prioritizes clarity and comparison. A header sets the context, while a sidebar provides quick access to filters or saved views. The main content area uses a comparison component to side-by-side display the two funds, highlighting critical metrics like returns, risk, and fees. A section with a timeline or audit log shows historical performance, and a card with a chart visualizes trends. A footer includes a recommendation button, ensuring the adviser can easily finalize their choice. The design keeps the most important data visible, supports quick scanning, and maintains a professional tone for financial decisions.
