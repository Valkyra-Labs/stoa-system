The screen is designed for a teacher to prepare for a meeting with a parent about a specific student. It provides an overview of the student's recent performance, behavior, and any relevant notes or documents. The layout is divided into three main sections:

1. **Student Summary**: A brief introduction to the student, including their name, grade, and a small photo.
2. **Recent Activity**: Displays a timeline of recent events related to the student, such as assignments, grades, and behavioral incidents.
3. **Preparation Notes**: A section for the teacher to add notes and review any documents or resources that may be useful during the meeting.

This layout ensures the teacher has all necessary information at hand to prepare effectively for the parent-teacher meeting.
