The main goal of the screen is to help a payments operations analyst resolve failed payouts efficiently by providing a clear, organized view of each failed payout and the actions needed to resolve it. The layout supports this goal by presenting a review queue where each failed payout is a card in a list. Each card includes key information about the failed payout, such as the payer, amount, and reason for failure. Below this information, the analyst can see and select from a list of possible actions to resolve the issue. This structure ensures that the analyst can focus on one failed payout at a time, understand the context, and take the necessary steps to resolve it without clutter or confusion.
