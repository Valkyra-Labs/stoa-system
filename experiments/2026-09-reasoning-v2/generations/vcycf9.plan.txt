The **"evidence and actions"** and **"order and emphasis"** rules shape this screen most. The teacher needs to see the most critical information first, such as the student's current performance, behavior, and any relevant history, to prepare effectively for the meeting. Evidence like grades, behavior reports, and teacher notes must be clearly visible and grouped logically, not hidden or buried. The screen should also highlight any urgent issues or actions needed before the meeting, such as follow-up tasks or interventions. The **"working with lists"** rule ensures that if multiple students are involved, the teacher can filter or view relevant data without losing context. The **"states"** rule ensures that if data is incomplete or delayed, it's clearly marked so the teacher knows to address it before the meeting.
