The key rules shaping this screen are "Order and emphasis" and "Numbers." The portfolio manager needs to quickly see the most significant changes in their portfolio since yesterday, so the list of changes should be sorted by the magnitude of the change, largest first. Each change must carry context, showing the basis (yesterday's value) to judge the significance, making Delta components essential. Emphasis should be clear, with the largest changes standing out without needing further explanation. Right-aligning numbers and showing units ensures clarity and ease of comparison, aiding quick decision-making.
