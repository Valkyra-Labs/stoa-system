1. The portfolio manager opens the screen daily to review market movements and understand the impact on their portfolio. This is a routine check to assess performance and make informed decisions.

2. The shape of the work is scanning a large collection for deviations. The manager reviews all assets but focuses on those that have moved significantly since yesterday.

3. The information includes all portfolio assets, their current value, change from yesterday, and reasons for the change. Values are judged against yesterday's performance and benchmarks.

4. The manager decides whether to rebalance or investigate unusual movements. Evidence includes change magnitude, reason for change, and historical performance.

5. The screen is structured with a header showing the date and purpose, a section listing all assets with their changes, and a section for detailed analysis of significant movements. The structure follows the scanning and decision-making process.
