The rule regarding **Order and emphasis** shapes this screen most significantly. The portfolio manager needs immediate clarity on risk, so exceptions (large negative deltas) must appear before average movements. Lists of changes must be sorted by the magnitude of the change, largest first, to highlight the most critical deviations instantly.

The **Numbers** rule ensures every metric displays its basis (e.g., yesterday's value, target, or peer average) so the manager understands *why* a number is significant without guessing. **Working with lists** dictates that filters persisting during the morning review and bulk actions for rebalancing large positions remain accessible. Finally, **Evidence and actions** require that the reason for a large move (e.g., a specific stock event) is visible immediately next to the delta, avoiding the need to drill down before understanding the context. Together, these rules create a high-signal, low-noise view focused on actionable anomalies.
