The main goal of the screen is to help a payments operations analyst efficiently resolve failed payouts by providing a clear overview of the issues and actionable tools to address them. The layout supports this by organizing the content into a structured flow: a header with the task title and filters, a table of failed payouts with key details, a sidebar for navigation and bulk actions, and a detail view for individual payouts. This structure enables the analyst to quickly scan, filter, and resolve issues while maintaining context and control over the workflow.
