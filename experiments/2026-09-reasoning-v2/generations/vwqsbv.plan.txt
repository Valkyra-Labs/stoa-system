The screen is designed for a payments operations analyst to resolve a list of failed payouts. It presents each failed payout in turn, allowing the analyst to review details, update the status, and add notes. The layout consists of a header with a title and actions, a main section divided into a primary area showing the current failed payout and its details, and a sidebar providing context and a history of actions taken. The analyst can move through the list of failed payouts sequentially, making decisions and recording resolutions for each one.
