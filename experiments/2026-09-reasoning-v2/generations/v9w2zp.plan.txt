The screen is shaped most by the rules of **order and emphasis**, **numbers**, and **states**. The finance director needs the most critical numbers upfront, so **exceptions and actions** are prioritized over averages. Numbers are **right-aligned** and **labeled with units and timeframes**, ensuring clarity. **StaleData** is shown if the data is outdated, helping the director know when numbers are current. The layout avoids decorative elements, focusing on **key metrics** and **actionable insights**. A **FilterBar** lets the director shape the data, and **BulkActions** enable quick decisions on multiple items. The screen avoids KPI cards and wraps data in **Cards** only when necessary, keeping it clean and focused on the most relevant financial indicators.
