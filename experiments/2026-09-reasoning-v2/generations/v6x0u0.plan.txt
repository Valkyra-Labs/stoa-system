The main goal of the screen is to provide a clear and concise overview of the school's performance for the board, highlighting key metrics and trends without requiring any interaction. The layout should be simple and easy to scan, with the most important information at the top. A header will name the school and the report period. Below the header, a series of cards will display critical metrics such as enrollment numbers, student performance, and financial health. Each card will contain a metric value, trend line, and brief description. This structure ensures that the board can quickly grasp the school's current status and recent performance trends.
