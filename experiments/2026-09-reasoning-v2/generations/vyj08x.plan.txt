The main goal of the screen is to present a clear and concise overview of the school's performance for the current term to the board. The layout should prioritize key metrics and recent activities to provide a comprehensive yet easily digestible view. A top Header will introduce the school and the term. Below it, a Section will display critical metrics such as enrollment numbers, attendance rates, and academic performance trends. Another Section will house a Timeline of recent events and announcements. This layout ensures that the most important information is readily accessible and well-organized, facilitating a smooth presentation.
