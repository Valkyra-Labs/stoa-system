The screen for the head of school to present the school's performance to the board should prioritize clarity, context, and completeness. Key rules include:

1. **Order and Emphasis**: Prioritize showing exceptions and significant changes first, ensuring the most critical metrics are immediately apparent.
2. **Numbers**: Include the basis for every number, such as previous period values or targets, to provide context for the metrics.
3. **Working with Lists**: Ensure persistent filters and clear navigation for detailed views without losing context.
4. **Evidence and Actions**: Present evidence directly next to metrics, ensuring transparency and supporting the narrative of the presentation.
5. **States**: Design for completeness by including states for empty, partial, stale, and error data, ensuring the presentation is robust and reliable.

These rules ensure the screen is informative, contextually rich, and reliable, aligning with the head of school’s need to present a comprehensive and clear overview of the school’s performance.
