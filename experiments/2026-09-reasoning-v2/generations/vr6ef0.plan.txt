The screen is a **dashboard** for the head of school to present the school's performance to the board. It provides a clear, visual summary of key metrics, trends, and comparisons, ensuring the board understands the school's strengths, areas for improvement, and overall progress. The layout is structured as a **Dashboard**, with **metrics** displayed prominently at the top, followed by **charts** and **tables** for deeper insights. A **sidebar** offers navigation to different sections, and a **footer** includes contextual details like the school year and data sources. Everything is static and unchangeable, as the head of school will not alter the screen before the presentation.
