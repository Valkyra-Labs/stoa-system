This screen is a high-level performance dashboard designed for a school board meeting, providing a clear, immutable snapshot of the school's annual achievements. It avoids interactive elements to ensure a static presentation flow.

The layout features a persistent header with the term title and school name. Below, a two-column main area presents key metrics side-by-side using the `Metric` and `Delta` components to highlight growth or decline. A large `Chart` component visualizes student enrollment or test score trends over the year. Beneath the charts, a concise `DataTable` summarizes specific program highlights or funding allocation details. The footer includes an `AuditLog` section showing recent strategic approvals to add context. The design prioritizes readability and trust, using neutral colors and clear typography suitable for a formal boardroom setting.
