The rules that shape this screen most are:  
- **Order and emphasis**: Exceptions and actionable items (failed payouts) are prioritized over averages, ensuring the analyst sees what needs attention first.  
- **Numbers**: All values (e.g., payout amounts, error codes) are clearly labeled with units and context, avoiding ambiguity.  
- **Working with lists**: Context is preserved by showing failed payouts in a list with a detail view open beside it, not replacing it. Filters and bulk actions are persistent, enabling efficient triage.  
- **Evidence and actions**: Each failed payout includes evidence (e.g., error logs, timestamps) alongside actions (e.g., retry, investigate), ensuring decisions are informed and reversible.  
These rules ensure clarity, efficiency, and accountability in resolving failed payouts.
