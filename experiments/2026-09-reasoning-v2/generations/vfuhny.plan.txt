1. **Session Context**: The portfolio manager opens their book daily in the morning to review performance changes since the previous day. They are looking for significant movements that require further analysis or action.

2. **Shape of the Work**: The primary task is to scan a large collection of assets for significant deviations from expected performance. The focus is on identifying outliers and understanding the reasons behind their movement.

3. **Information**: The screen must display each asset's performance metrics (e.g., price, volume, volatility) since the previous day. Comparative measures include the previous day’s performance and any set targets or thresholds.

4. **Decision and Evidence**: The decision involves understanding why specific assets moved significantly and determining if further action is necessary. Evidence includes detailed performance data, historical trends, and any relevant news or events.

5. **Structure**: The screen should have a top-level summary of overall portfolio performance, followed by a detailed list of assets with significant movements. Each asset should have a section for detailed analysis and supporting evidence. The structure should prioritize quick scanning and easy access to detailed information.

6. **Components**: Use a combination of `Page`, `Header`, `Section`, `Card`, `Comparison`, `Chart`, and `DataTable` to present the data. A `Comparison` component can show side-by-side metrics for each asset, while a `Chart` component can display historical trends. A `DataTable` can list assets with significant movements, and `Card` components can provide detailed analysis for each asset.
