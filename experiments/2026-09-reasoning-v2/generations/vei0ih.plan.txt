The **order and emphasis** and **evidence and actions** rules shape this screen most. The teacher needs to prepare for a focused, meaningful conversation, so the most important information—such as the student's performance, behavior, and progress—must be **obvious and prioritized**. The screen should **show the evidence** for any observations or recommendations, like specific examples of work or behavior, so the teacher can confidently explain their judgment. **Consequences of actions** (like recommending a tutor or changing a behavior plan) should be clear, with **confirmation steps** for irreversible decisions. The screen should also **keep context**—showing the student's history and current status—so the teacher can make informed, empathetic decisions during the meeting.
