The screen is designed for a payments operations analyst to resolve failed payouts. It presents a queue of failed payout attempts, each with essential details such as the payment ID, recipient, and reason for failure. The analyst can review each item, update relevant information, and mark the payout as resolved or return it to processing. The layout consists of a sidebar for navigation, a main area with a list of failed payouts, and a detail view for each payout where the analyst can make decisions and update status.
