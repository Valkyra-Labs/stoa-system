The screen is a **ReviewQueue** for resolving failed payouts. It displays a list of failed transactions, each with details like amount, reason for failure, and payment method. The layout includes a **FilterBar** for searching or filtering by date, payout method, or failure reason. A **DataTable** shows the list of failed payouts, with columns for key information and a **BulkActions** bar for resolving multiple items at once. The **current** item is highlighted, and the analyst can make a **decision** (e.g., retry, refund, or cancel) for each. The interface supports efficient resolution of failed payouts while providing context and actionable options.
