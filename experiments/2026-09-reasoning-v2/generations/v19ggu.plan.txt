For this screen, the rules that shape it most are "Order and emphasis" and "Numbers." Since the head of school is presenting the current state of the school and no changes will be made, the emphasis should be on clear, unambiguous presentation of key metrics and their baselines. The most important metrics should be immediately visible without additional navigation. Numbers must be right-aligned in tables, accompanied by their units and time frames, and freshness should be indicated for any live or imported data. This ensures the board receives a comprehensive, easy-to-understand overview of the school's performance.
