### Steps 1 to 5 Summary

1. **Session Context**: A financial adviser opens this screen to compare two investment funds and make a recommendation to a client. The adviser is responding to a client request or an update from the financial institution.

2. **Work Shape**: The adviser is setting two objects (funds) against each other to make a decision. There are two primary entities (funds) in play, and the decision is made on this screen.

3. **Information**: The screen must display key metrics and performance data for both funds, including historical returns, risk levels, and recent performance. Each value should be judged against a target or a peer (the other fund).

4. **Decision and Evidence**: The adviser decides which fund to recommend based on a comparison of key metrics and performance. The decision evidence includes detailed metrics, risk assessments, and performance trends.

5. **Structure**: The screen should have a header with the client's name and the task at hand. Below the header, a side-by-side comparison of the two funds should be visible, with key metrics and performance data. A timeline of recent performance events should be included. The decision area should be at the bottom, with a summary of the comparison and a place to input the recommendation.

Given these steps, the **structure** will include:
- A header with the client's name and task.
- A side-by-side comparison of the two funds.
- A timeline of performance events.
- A decision area with a summary and recommendation input.

### Components:
- **Header**: Displays the client's name and the task.
- **Compare**: For side-by-side comparison of the two funds.
- **Timeline**: To show recent performance events.
- **Card**: For the decision area with summary and recommendation input.
