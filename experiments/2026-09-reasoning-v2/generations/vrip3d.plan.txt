The most shaping rules are **Order and emphasis** and **Evidence and actions**. The screen must prioritize items needing immediate action (exceptions) over stable averages, sorting changes by magnitude to highlight volatility. Crucially, every decision to trade or close a position requires visible evidence next to the action; reasons, history, and sources cannot be hidden. This ensures the portfolio manager understands *why* a move occurred before acting. Additionally, **Numbers** rule dictates that every metric must show its basis (e.g., yesterday’s close, target, or peer delta) so judgments are contextualized. The **Interface rules** against decorative regions mean the layout is dense and functional, focusing solely on the data required to diagnose market shifts and execute trades efficiently.
