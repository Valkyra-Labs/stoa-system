The main goal is to provide a clear, authoritative, and instantly comprehensible snapshot of the school's performance for the board, ensuring the head of school can present without needing to explain the dashboard.

The layout prioritizes the most critical metrics immediately at the top to answer "how are we doing?" within seconds. A high-level executive summary uses large, clear numbers to show overall health. Below this, a dedicated section visualizes trends over time, allowing the presenter to point to growth or decline. A secondary section details operational health (e.g., attendance, finance) with concise data tables for drill-down facts. Finally, a clean audit log or key risk indicator section ensures transparency. The design avoids clutter, uses consistent spacing to group related concepts, and employs plain language labels so the message is understood by non-technical board members immediately.
