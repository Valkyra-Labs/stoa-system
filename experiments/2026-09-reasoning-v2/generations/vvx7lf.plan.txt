**Main Goal:** Provide the finance director with a clear, concise overview of the company's weekly financial performance to inform Monday meetings, enabling quick decisions and discussions.

**Layout Support:** The screen is structured to prioritize key metrics at the top for immediate insight, with supporting data and trends below. A header sets the context, and a sidebar offers quick access to detailed reports or filters. Sections group related data (e.g., revenue, expenses, cash flow), while charts and tables offer visual and numerical clarity. This layout ensures the finance director can grasp the big picture quickly and dive into specifics when needed, all in a clean, focused interface.
