The screen serves as a morning dashboard for a portfolio manager to quickly assess the performance and movements of their investment portfolio since the previous day. The layout is designed to provide a clear and concise overview, divided into several key sections:

1. **Overview Metrics**: A row of metrics showing the portfolio's overall performance indicators such as total value, gains/losses, and key ratios.
2. **Top Movers**: A section highlighting the most significant changes in individual assets, including stocks, bonds, and other securities, with brief explanations of market events affecting them.
3. **Performance Charts**: A set of charts displaying the portfolio's performance over the past 24 hours, including line charts for total value and pie charts for asset distribution.
4. **Review Log**: A log of significant events and actions taken in the portfolio, such as trades or adjustments, along with comments on market conditions.
5. **Action Items**: A list of actionable insights and next steps based on the day's performance and market conditions.

This layout ensures that the portfolio manager can quickly grasp the critical information needed to make informed decisions for the day.
