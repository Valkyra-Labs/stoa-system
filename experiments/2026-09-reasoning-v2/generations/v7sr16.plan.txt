The main goal of the screen is to provide a quick, at-a-glance overview of key financial metrics for the company over the past week, enabling the finance director to prepare for Monday meetings efficiently. The layout is designed to prioritize clarity and speed of comprehension, with the most critical metrics prominently displayed at the top. Key financial indicators such as revenue, expenses, profit margins, and cash flow are shown using Metric and Trend components to highlight recent performance trends. Supporting charts and tables offer deeper insights but are secondary, ensuring the finance director can grasp the essential information without distraction.
