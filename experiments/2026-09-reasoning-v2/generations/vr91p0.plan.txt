The screen is shaped most by the **order and emphasis** rule, which places exceptions and actionable items first. The portfolio manager needs to see what has moved since yesterday, so the most significant changes are shown upfront. The **numbers** rule also applies, requiring clear display of values, deltas, and their bases for informed decisions. The **evidence and actions** rule ensures that reasons for changes are shown alongside the data, not hidden, so the manager can understand why things moved. The **working with lists** rule keeps context by showing a list of assets with their changes, while the **states** rule ensures the screen handles stale or partial data gracefully. The layout avoids KPI cards and wraps data in meaningful regions like Comparison and DataTable.
