The screen is designed for a payments operations analyst to review and resolve a list of failed payouts. The layout consists of a sidebar with navigation links and a main area divided into three sections: a toolbar with filters to narrow down the list, a table displaying the failed payouts, and a detail panel showing information about the currently selected payout. The toolbar allows the analyst to apply filters such as date range and reason for failure. The table lists the failed payouts with options to select and view details. The detail panel provides additional information about the selected payout, such as the recipient details, reason for failure, and actions to resolve the issue.
