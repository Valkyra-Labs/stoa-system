The main goal of the screen is to provide a quick, high-level overview of the company's financial performance for the past week. This allows the finance director to understand key trends, identify any anomalies, and prepare for meetings. The layout supports this goal by placing the most critical information at the top, with clear, concise metrics and visualizations that highlight recent changes and significant figures. Sections are organized to show performance metrics first, followed by detailed charts and tables for deeper analysis if needed. This structure ensures the finance director can grasp the essential financial status with minimal effort and dive deeper only when necessary.
