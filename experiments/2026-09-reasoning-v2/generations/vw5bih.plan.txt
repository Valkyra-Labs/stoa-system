The main goal is to enable the analyst to efficiently identify, investigate, and resolve failed payouts while maintaining full auditability. The layout prioritizes a clear, high-density list of failures at the top, allowing immediate scanning of root causes (e.g., "insufficient funds," "merchant declined"). A dedicated "Resolution" column with bulk actions and inline editing minimizes context switching. Below the list, a detailed "Inspector" view opens to show transaction history and merchant context only when needed, preventing clutter. This structure ensures the most critical data (the failure list) is always visible, secondary context is accessible on demand, and actions are grouped logically for rapid workflow completion.
