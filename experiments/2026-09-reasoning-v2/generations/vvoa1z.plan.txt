The screen is a weekly dashboard for a finance director to quickly review key financial metrics and trends before meetings. It includes a summary of the most important numbers, such as revenue, expenses, and profit, along with visual charts to highlight changes over the past week. The layout consists of a header with the title and a brief subtitle, a main section divided into three parts: a card for key metrics, a chart for revenue trends, and a table for detailed financial data. This setup allows the finance director to grasp the financial health and recent performance of the company at a glance.
