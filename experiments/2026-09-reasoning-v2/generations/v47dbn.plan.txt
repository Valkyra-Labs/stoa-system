This screen allows a financial adviser to compare two funds and make a recommendation based on a unified dashboard. The layout is split into three primary sections. At the top, a **Header** displays the client's name and the two funds being compared, with action buttons to export the report or save the comparison. Below this, a **Section** presents a **Comparison** view side-by-side, showing shared metrics like expense ratios, risk levels, and projected returns for both funds. 

Underneath, a **Section** breaks down the details for each fund individually using **Card** containers, each containing an **Inspector** pattern to show historical performance trends and recent audit logs. Finally, a **Section** at the bottom provides a **ReviewQueue** interface where the adviser can toggle between funds, view exception signals for any red flags, and record their final recommendation directly on the screen. The entire flow is guided by a persistent **FilterBar** to adjust the comparison parameters without losing context.
