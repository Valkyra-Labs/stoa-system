1. The analyst is a payments operations professional who regularly reviews payout failures to resolve issues. They open this screen to address a batch of failed transactions, likely triggered by a system alert or routine check.

2. The shape of the work is taking each item in turn to a decision, until none are left. The analyst reviews each failed payout, investigates the cause, and resolves it, often making decisions on this screen.

3. The entities are failed payout records, with measures like payout status, error code, and amount. Each value is judged against a target (successful payout) and a basis (previous attempts). Values like error code and timestamp are critical for decision-making.

4. The analyst decides whether to retry, refund, or investigate further. They need to see the error code, timestamp, and any previous attempts to make an informed decision. A wrong decision could lead to customer dissatisfaction or financial loss, but some actions can be reversed.

5. The screen should start with a list of failed payouts, showing key metrics and error codes. A sidebar or drawer can provide details on each payout, with filters to narrow down the list. A bulk action section allows the analyst to resolve multiple payouts at once.
