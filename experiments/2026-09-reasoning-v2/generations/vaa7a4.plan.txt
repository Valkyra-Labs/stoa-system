The main goal of the screen is to help a financial adviser compare two investment funds and recommend the most suitable one to a client. The layout should clearly present key metrics and details of both funds side by side, allowing for easy comparison. At the top, a header identifies the purpose of the screen. Below it, a "Compare" pattern displays the two funds with their respective metrics, such as returns, risk levels, and fees. A "Decision" section at the bottom provides space for the adviser to summarize their recommendation and rationale, ensuring all relevant information is readily available and accessible for a well-informed decision.
