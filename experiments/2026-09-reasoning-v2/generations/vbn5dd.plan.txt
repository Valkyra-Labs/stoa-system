The main goal of the screen is to provide a quick overview of the significant movements in the portfolio since the previous day, along with the reasons behind these changes. The layout is designed to support this by placing the most critical information at the top, followed by supporting details. The screen features a header that names the portfolio and the time range being viewed. Below the header, a chart displays the overall movement of the portfolio, immediately drawing attention to the most impactful changes. A table follows, listing the specific assets that have moved significantly, with details on their performance and contributing factors. This layout ensures the portfolio manager can quickly grasp the day's critical movements and delve into specifics as needed.
