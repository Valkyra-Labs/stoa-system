The screen is a **Dashboard** for the head of school to present the school's performance to the board. It provides an overview of key metrics and visualizations that show the school's progress, challenges, and achievements. The layout is structured into **sections**, each highlighting a specific area like student performance, staff efficiency, budget usage, and safety. Each section includes **cards** with **metrics**, **charts**, and **tables** for detailed data. A **sidebar** offers quick access to filters and saved views, while the **main content** is organized into **sections** with **headers** and **cards** for clarity and visual hierarchy. The design ensures the data is easy to understand and visually engaging for the board.
