This screen assists a financial adviser in selecting between two funds by presenting a side-by-side comparison of their performance and risk profiles. The layout is split into two primary columns, one for each fund, with a central divider showing the comparative metrics. At the top, a header displays the selected pair of funds and key summary tags (e.g., "High Growth" vs. "Stable Income"). Below, each column contains a card for the fund's name and ticker, followed by a dashboard section with metrics (returns, volatility) and small trend charts. A "Recommendation" section at the bottom offers a quick summary of why one might be chosen over the other, along with action buttons to save the comparison or export the report. The design emphasizes clarity and quick decision-making, allowing the adviser to visually contrast the two options without clutter.
