**1. User and Session:** A payments operations analyst reviews a list of failed payouts daily to ensure timely resolution. They open this screen to address specific failures flagged by the system.

**2. Shape of Work:** The analyst scans a list of items, taking each one in turn to a decision, until no unresolved items remain.

**3. Information:** The screen must show a list of failed payouts, their details, and relevant metadata like the reason for failure, the payout amount, and the associated merchant.

**4. Decision and Evidence:** The analyst decides whether to approve or reject each failed payout. Evidence includes the failure reason, payout amount, and any associated comments or documents.

**5. Structure:** The screen should have a list of failed payouts, with each item expandable to show detailed information. A decision area should be adjacent to each item for marking it as resolved. A progress indicator shows the number of resolved and unresolved items.

Components: Use the `ReviewQueue` pattern with `items` representing failed payouts, `current` for the detailed view, and `decision` for approving or rejecting.
