**Main Goal:** The screen helps the teacher prepare for a parent-teacher meeting by organizing key student information, observations, and discussion points in a clear, structured way.

**Layout Support:** The layout prioritizes the student's profile and recent performance at the top for quick reference. A sidebar provides quick access to notes and action items, while the main content area includes a summary of academic and behavioral progress, along with suggested discussion points. This structure ensures the teacher can review all relevant details efficiently before the meeting, maintaining focus and clarity during the conversation.
