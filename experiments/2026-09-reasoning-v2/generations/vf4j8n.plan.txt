### Main Goal
The screen's primary goal is to provide the Head of School with a single, undeniable view of the school's performance for the upcoming board presentation. It must synthesize complex operational data into a clear, authoritative narrative that highlights achievements, identifies key trends, and justifies the term's outcome. The design prioritizes readability and impact over interactivity, ensuring the data tells a compelling story immediately.

### Layout Strategy
The layout supports this goal through a strict hierarchy of information:
1.  **Executive Summary Header**: A prominent top section displaying the school name, term date, and high-level aggregate metrics (e.g., attendance, average score) using large, clear `Metric` components.
2.  **Trend Narrative**: A central `Dashboard` section featuring `Chart` components to visualize growth and stability over the term, allowing the audience to see progress at a glance.
3.  **Evidence Table**: Below the charts, a focused `DataTable` provides the granular details (student cohorts, specific departments) required to back up the high-level claims, with clear row labels and status indicators.
4.  **Supporting Context**: A secondary sidebar or bottom panel offers `AuditLog` snippets for accountability and `Status` indicators for critical operational health, ensuring transparency without distracting from the main narrative.

This structure moves the audience from the "what" (metrics) to the "how" (charts) to the "why" (table details), creating a logical flow for a high-stakes presentation.
