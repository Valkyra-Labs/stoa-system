This screen serves as the "Morning Briefing" for a portfolio manager, designed to instantly surface portfolio value changes and the reasons behind them. It features a top header with the portfolio name and a date range selector. Below, a dashboard-style card displays key metrics like total P&L, unrealized gains, and sector exposure shifts, accompanied by small trend indicators.

The core of the screen is a large, sticky-table view of the portfolio holdings. Rows highlight positions with significant overnight movement. Hovering or clicking a row opens a side drawer (Inspector pattern) detailing specific reasons for the change: news headlines, macro shifts, or fundamental updates. Above the table, a filter bar allows the manager to sort by delta magnitude or sector. The layout prioritizes quick scanning of winners/losers first, followed by deep-dive analysis via the drawer, ensuring the manager understands both the "what" and the "why" immediately.
