The screen is a **ReviewQueue** for resolving failed payouts. It shows a list of failed transactions, each with details like amount, status, and reason. The layout includes a **FilterBar** for filtering by date, payout method, or reason, and a **SavedViews** menu for quick access to common filters. Each payout is displayed in a **DataTable** with selectable rows, and a **BulkActions** bar allows applying actions like "Retry" or "Escalate" to multiple items. The **current** item is highlighted, and the **decision** area lets the analyst resolve the issue. The **toolbar** includes a **Button** to add new failed payouts. The design is clean, focused, and efficient for batch processing.
