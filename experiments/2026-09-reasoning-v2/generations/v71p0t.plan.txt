**Main goal**: Provide the finance director with a quick overview of the company's weekly financial performance to inform strategic discussions and decisions before meetings. The layout supports this by prioritizing key metrics and trends in a clear, scannable format, allowing the director to grasp the most important data at a glance without needing to dive into detailed reports.

**Layout support**: The screen is structured to highlight the most relevant financial indicators—revenue, expenses, profit, and key variances—using cards and charts for visual clarity. A summary of the week’s performance is placed at the top, followed by a timeline of major events and a comparison of this week’s numbers against the previous week. This arrangement ensures the director can quickly assess the financial health of the company and prepare for meetings.
