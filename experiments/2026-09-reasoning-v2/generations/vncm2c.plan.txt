The screen is designed for a payments operations analyst to review and resolve failed payouts. It presents a queue of failed payout attempts, each with essential details like the payout ID, recipient, and reason for failure. The analyst can navigate through each item, view context about the failure, and decide on the next action, such as retrying the payout or marking it as resolved with a note. The layout includes a sidebar for navigation and quick access to common actions, a main area for the payout queue, and a detailed view of the selected payout item.
