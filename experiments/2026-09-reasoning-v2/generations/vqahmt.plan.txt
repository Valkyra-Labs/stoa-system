This screen serves as the Finance Director's "Monday Morning Command Center," designed to provide immediate clarity on weekly financial performance before board meetings. It is a single-page dashboard with a persistent sidebar for quick navigation to historical data or specific departments.

The layout features a prominent header displaying the current week's date and key alerts. Below, a high-level metric summary uses large numbers and trend indicators to show revenue, expenses, and cash flow. A central section houses an interactive bar chart comparing actual vs. budgeted figures across business units. Beneath the chart, a concise data table lists top-performing and under-performing departments, with a filter bar to drill down into specific accounts. A secondary panel on the right highlights "exceptions"—variances exceeding a set threshold—allowing for rapid risk assessment. The design prioritizes readability and speed, ensuring the director can grasp the financial health of the company in under two minutes.
