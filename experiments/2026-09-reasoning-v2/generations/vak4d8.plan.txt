The main goal of the screen is to help a financial adviser compare two funds and make a clear, informed recommendation. The layout supports this by presenting the funds side-by-side in a structured, easy-to-scan format. Key metrics like performance, risk, and fees are highlighted upfront, allowing for quick comparison. A clear summary of the adviser’s recommendation is placed prominently at the top, followed by supporting data and analysis. This structure ensures the adviser can focus on the most relevant information without unnecessary distractions, making the decision process efficient and transparent.
