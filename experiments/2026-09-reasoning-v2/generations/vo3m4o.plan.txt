**Main Goal:** The screen provides the head of school with a clear, concise overview of the school's performance across key areas, enabling them to confidently present the term's progress to the board without any changes.

**Layout Support:** The layout is structured to prioritize key metrics at the top, using cards and metrics components for quick scanning. A header sets the context, while sections group related data like student performance, staff engagement, and financial health. A sidebar offers quick access to detailed reports, and a timeline highlights major events. This structure ensures the head of school can quickly grasp the school's status and prepare for the board meeting without needing to navigate through complex data.
