The **"Working with lists"** and **"Evidence and actions"** rules shape this screen most. The analyst needs to review a list of failed payouts, so the screen includes a **FilterBar** to persist filters while working and a **DataTable** to show the list, with context preserved when drilling into details. **BulkActions** are offered for common resolutions like retrying or contacting merchants. Each failed payout shows its **evidence**, such as error codes, timestamps, and payment details, next to actionable buttons. **StaleData** or **ErrorData** states are shown if data is outdated or unavailable. The **"Order and emphasis"** rule ensures the most urgent failures (e.g., high-value or time-sensitive) are prioritized, and **"Numbers"** rule ensures all relevant values are clearly labeled with units and timeframes.
