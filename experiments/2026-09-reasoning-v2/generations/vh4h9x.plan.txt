The screen is a static dashboard for a head of school to present to the board, showcasing the school's performance metrics for the term. It is designed to be clear and concise, providing an overview of key performance indicators without any interactive elements. The layout consists of a header with the school's name and a subtitle indicating the term being reviewed. Below the header, the dashboard is divided into two main sections: a metrics section on the left displaying key figures such as enrollment numbers, attendance rates, and academic performance indicators, and a charts section on the right showing trends and comparisons over time.
