**Main goal**: The screen should help the portfolio manager quickly identify which assets have moved since yesterday and understand the reasons behind those changes, enabling timely decisions.

**Layout support**: The layout prioritizes clarity and focus by placing the most critical information at the top—such as a summary of key movements and their causes. A **Header** with a title and summary actions provides context, while a **Section** or **Card** displays the **Trend** and **Delta** of key assets. A **Comparison** view shows performance against benchmarks, and a **Timeline** or **AuditLog** explains the reasons for changes. A **DataTable** or **Explorer** allows deeper inspection of individual assets. This structure ensures the manager can scan, understand, and act on changes efficiently.
