**Steps 1 to 5 Summary:**

1. **Session Context:** A payments operations analyst reviews and resolves failed payouts daily. They open this screen to address a list of failed transactions flagged by the system.

2. **Shape of Work:** The analyst works through a queue of failed transactions, one by one, taking actions to resolve each issue.

3. **Information:** The screen needs to display a list of failed payouts with key details like payout ID, recipient, amount, and reason for failure. Each item should also show relevant timestamps and status updates.

4. **Decision and Evidence:** The analyst decides how to resolve each failed payout (e.g., retry, escalate, or mark as invalid). They need detailed transaction history, error codes, and contact information to make informed decisions.

5. **Structure:** The screen should have a queue of failed payouts (ReviewQueue pattern), with each item showing critical details and a history of actions. A sidebar or secondary panel provides additional information and actions for each item. A main area displays the current item's details, and a bottom section shows action buttons (Retry, Escalate, Mark as Invalid).

This structure ensures the analyst sees all necessary information for each decision and can take action directly from the screen.
