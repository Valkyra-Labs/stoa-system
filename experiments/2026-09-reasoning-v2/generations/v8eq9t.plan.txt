The key rules shaping this screen are "Order and emphasis," "Working with lists," and "Evidence and actions." The screen prioritizes the failed payouts (exceptions) to ensure the analyst addresses them first. It maintains context by showing the list alongside the details of each failed payout. Persistent filters and bulk actions streamline the workflow. Each failed payout includes evidence like error codes, timestamps, and transaction details directly next to the resolution options, ensuring the analyst can see the cause and take appropriate action immediately. Confirmation steps are provided for any actions that could be irreversible.
