The rules that shape this screen most are **Order and Emphasis** and **Evidence and Actions**. The financial adviser needs to quickly identify which fund is the better choice, so the most important information should be immediately apparent. This involves showing the key metrics and performance data side by side, emphasizing the differences in a clear and concise manner. Additionally, the screen must provide the evidence necessary to support the recommendation, such as historical performance, risk ratings, and any relevant financial metrics. Each fund should have a clear call-to-action button, accompanied by a confirmation of the action's consequence, to guide the adviser in making and documenting the recommendation.
