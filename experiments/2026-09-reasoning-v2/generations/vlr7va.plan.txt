The main goal of this screen is to provide a clear, prioritized view of portfolio performance changes since the previous day, enabling the manager to quickly identify underperforming or volatile assets and understand the drivers behind those movements.

The layout supports this by placing high-level summary metrics at the very top, using color-coded cards to highlight significant gains or losses immediately. Below this, a filtered table displays detailed daily changes for all positions, allowing the manager to sort by magnitude of change. To explain the "why," the screen includes an integrated audit log or drill-down capability next to each significant row, showing specific market events, rebalancing actions, or external data updates. A sidebar offers quick navigation to specific asset classes or filters for deep dives, ensuring the most critical information is always visible without requiring multiple screen switches.
