The main goal of the screen is to help a portfolio manager quickly understand the significant movements in their investment portfolio since the previous day. The layout is designed to provide a clear, concise overview of the most impactful changes, followed by detailed insights into why these changes occurred. At the top, a header names the screen and shows essential navigation. Below it, a **Metric** component highlights the overall portfolio performance, immediately drawing attention to the most critical information. A **Comparison** component follows, displaying the top movers in the portfolio side by side, making it easy to compare and contrast. Beneath that, a **Chart** shows the trend of these movements over time, offering context and deeper insights. Finally, an **AuditLog** lists recent transactions and actions, helping the manager trace the reasons behind the changes. This layout ensures the portfolio manager can quickly grasp the day's most significant developments and investigate further if needed.
