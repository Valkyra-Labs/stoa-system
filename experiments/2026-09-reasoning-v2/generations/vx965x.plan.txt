**Main Goal:** Help the teacher prepare for a parent-teacher meeting by providing relevant student information, key discussion points, and action items.

**Layout Support:** The screen is structured to guide the teacher through preparation in a logical flow. A header sets the context (student name and meeting date). A sidebar provides quick access to student data like grades, behavior, and attendance. The main content area includes a summary of the student's performance, key discussion points, and suggested action items. A section for notes allows the teacher to plan what to say. A footer includes a reminder and a button to save or send the meeting summary, ensuring the teacher is well-prepared and focused.
