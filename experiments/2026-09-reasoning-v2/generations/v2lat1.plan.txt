The rules that shape this screen most are: **Order and emphasis**, **Numbers**, and **States**. The head of school needs to present a clear, focused overview of the school's performance, so the most important metrics (like attendance, academic outcomes, and safety) are emphasized upfront. Numbers are shown with their basis (e.g., targets, previous terms) and units, ensuring clarity for the board. The screen must also account for data freshness (StaleData) and handle partial or missing data gracefully. These rules ensure the board can quickly grasp key performance indicators, understand the context of each number, and see if data is current or needs attention—all without unnecessary complexity or distractions.
