The most critical rules for this screen are "Order and emphasis" and "Evidence and actions." Given that the financial adviser must recommend one of two funds, the screen should prioritize the most important information to facilitate an informed decision. This involves highlighting key metrics and trends that differentiate the funds, ensuring the most impactful data is immediately visible. Additionally, the screen must provide clear evidence for each decision, such as historical performance, risk levels, and return rates, alongside each fund's summary.
