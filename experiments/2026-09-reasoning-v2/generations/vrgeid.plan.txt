The main goal is to empower the teacher with a consolidated, easy-to-scan view of a single student's context before the meeting, ensuring all relevant data is accessible in one place. The layout prioritizes hierarchy: a prominent header identifies the student, followed by a "Quick Summary" card for immediate emotional and academic context. Supporting sections are organized logically—recent behavior and academics first, then specific concerns and goals, and finally a chronological log of recent events. This structure guides the teacher from high-level impressions to specific details, reducing cognitive load and preventing the need to jump between multiple tabs. The design emphasizes clarity over density, using distinct sections and clear labels to separate different aspects of the student's life, allowing the teacher to mentally organize the conversation points before walking into the room.
