The main goal is to guide the financial adviser through a structured comparison of two funds, culminating in a clear recommendation. The layout prioritizes the decision-making process by placing the side-by-side fund comparison at the top, allowing immediate visual assessment of key metrics like returns and risk. Below this, a detailed breakdown section lets the adviser drill into specific characteristics of each fund, while a dedicated action area at the bottom provides a single, prominent button to finalize the recommendation. This vertical flow moves the user from broad comparison to deep analysis and finally to a definitive action, ensuring the choice is well-informed and the process is efficient.
