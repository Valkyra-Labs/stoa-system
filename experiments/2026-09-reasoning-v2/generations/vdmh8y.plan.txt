The screen is a **Dashboard** that provides the head of school with a comprehensive overview of the school's performance across key metrics. It is designed to be static and unchanging for the presentation, so all data is pre-loaded and formatted for clarity. The layout is divided into **sections** such as "Student Performance," "Staff Engagement," and "Financial Health," each containing **metrics**, **charts**, and **tables** to show quantitative data. A **sidebar** offers quick access to filters and saved views, while a **header** displays the title and a brief summary. The design is clean, professional, and focused on data visualization to help the head of school present the school's status effectively.
