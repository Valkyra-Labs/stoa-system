The most crucial rules for this screen are **Order and Emphasis** and **Evidence and Actions**. The teacher needs to focus on the student's most significant issues and be prepared with evidence to support the discussion. The screen should prioritize the student's critical performance metrics and behavioral indicators, ensuring they are prominently displayed. Each actionable item or concern should come with supporting evidence or context, such as recent grades, attendance records, and behavioral notes, directly adjacent to the summary. This layout helps the teacher understand the student's situation thoroughly and be ready to discuss specific actions or interventions.
