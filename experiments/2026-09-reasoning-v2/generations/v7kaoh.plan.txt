The rules that shape this screen most are: **order and emphasis**, **numbers**, and **states**. The finance director needs the most critical numbers upfront, so exceptions and actionable metrics are shown first. Numbers are right-aligned and include units and time frames for clarity. Stale data is clearly marked with a timestamp, ensuring the director knows the freshness of the data. The screen avoids decorative elements and repeats, keeping it focused on key financial indicators. A **Dashboard** pattern is used to group metrics, charts, and tables, while **StaleData** and **ErrorData** states are included for transparency. The layout prioritizes the most important data for quick decision-making, aligning with the finance director's need to review weekly numbers efficiently.
