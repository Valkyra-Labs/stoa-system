The main goal of the screen is to help a payments operations analyst efficiently identify, resolve, and track failed payouts. The layout supports this by placing the most critical information—such as payout status, error messages, and resolution actions—prominently at the top. A table of failed payouts provides a clear overview, with filters and bulk actions to streamline the resolution process. A sidebar or detail panel offers additional context, such as transaction details and payment methods, while a status summary or audit log ensures transparency and traceability. This structure enables the analyst to focus on resolving issues quickly while maintaining an overview of the entire process.
