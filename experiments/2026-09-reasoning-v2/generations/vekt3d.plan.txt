The main goal of the screen is to provide the finance director with a quick, high-level overview of the company's financial performance for the past week. This includes key metrics such as revenue, expenses, profit, and any significant variances compared to previous weeks or targets. The layout is designed to be concise and easy to scan, ensuring that the most critical information is immediately visible at the top. The screen uses a combination of Metric and Trend components to display numerical data and trends, respectively. A Chart component may be included to visualize broader financial trends over time. This setup allows the finance director to quickly assess the company's financial health and identify any areas that require further investigation.
