The rules that most shape this screen are **Order and emphasis** (prioritizing exceptions and sorting changes by magnitude), **Numbers** (displaying deltas with explicit basis like yesterday's value or target), and **Evidence and actions** (showing the reason for movement immediately next to the change).

Because the user is a portfolio manager checking the morning book, they need to instantly identify which positions moved most significantly to understand market impact. The screen must present a sorted list of changes, with the largest movements at the top, avoiding a sea of minor noise. Each entry requires context: not just the new price, but the delta against yesterday's close (the basis) and the reason (e.g., "bid-ask spread widened"). The layout should be an **Explorer** or **DataTable** pattern, keeping the table visible while allowing deep inspection of specific positions without losing the list view. Decorative KPIs should be minimized in favor of actionable data; the focus is on the *why* and the *how much*, not just the absolute level.
