The main goal of the screen is to help a financial adviser evaluate and recommend one of two funds to a client by comparing their performance and features clearly. The layout supports this goal by placing the two funds side by side in the center of the screen, ensuring an easy comparison. Above the funds, a header briefly introduces the task. Below each fund, key metrics such as return rate, risk level, and expense ratio are displayed for quick reference. Additional details like historical performance charts and a brief description are accessible via tabs, allowing the adviser to dive deeper into each fund's characteristics as needed. This layout ensures that the most critical information is immediately visible, while providing easy access to more detailed analysis.
