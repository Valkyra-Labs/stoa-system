The main goal is to help an analyst efficiently identify, triage, and resolve failed payouts by reducing cognitive load and minimizing manual navigation. The layout prioritizes a high-density, sortable table of failures as the central focus, allowing rapid scanning and filtering. A prominent "Resolve All" action button provides immediate bulk handling for common issues. Supporting sections include a sticky audit log to track resolution history and a secondary pane for detailed error context without losing place in the list. This structure supports a linear, task-oriented workflow where the analyst moves from broad filtering to specific resolution, then to verification, keeping critical data always visible while hiding complexity behind context menus or detail drawers.
