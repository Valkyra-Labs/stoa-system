The screen provides the finance director with a quick overview of the company's weekly financial performance. It is laid out with a header showing the week's date and a summary of key metrics. On the left, a sidebar contains navigation to other financial reports and a list of upcoming meetings. The main area features a dashboard with metrics like revenue, expenses, and profit, displayed as cards. Below the metrics, a line chart shows weekly trends, and a table lists detailed line items. A filter bar allows the director to adjust the view by department or time frame, ensuring they can quickly assess the numbers before meetings.
