The **"Put the exceptions and the items needing action before the averages"** and **"Show at most one level of emphasis per region"** rules shape this screen most. The financial adviser needs to quickly identify which fund is better, so the most important metrics—like returns, risk, and fees—must be emphasized upfront. The screen should highlight the fund with the best performance and lowest risk first, while clearly showing the differences between the two. The **"Evidence and actions"** rule also applies, as the adviser needs to see the basis for each metric (e.g., annualized return vs. 10-year average) and understand the implications of choosing one fund over the other. This ensures the adviser can make an informed decision with all relevant data clearly presented.
