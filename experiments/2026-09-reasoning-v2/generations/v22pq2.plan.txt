The main goal of this screen is to provide the Finance Director with an immediate, high-level view of the company's financial health at a glance, enabling rapid decision-making before the Monday meeting.

The layout prioritizes speed and clarity by placing the most critical metrics—such as revenue, expenses, and net profit—in prominent cards at the top. A comparative section follows to show month-over-month trends and variances against budget, highlighting deviations that require attention. Below, a concise table lists key transaction types or departmental summaries, allowing for quick verification of specific data points. The design avoids clutter, using whitespace and clear headings to separate these information layers, ensuring the director can confirm the week's performance and identify urgent issues within seconds of opening the screen.
