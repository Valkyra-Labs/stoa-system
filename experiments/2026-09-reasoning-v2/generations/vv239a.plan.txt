The rules that shape this screen most are: **"Put the exceptions and the items needing action before the averages"**, **"Sort lists of changes by the size of the change"**, and **"Show at most one level of emphasis per region"**. The finance director needs to quickly identify anomalies and urgent issues in the weekly numbers. The screen prioritizes high-impact changes and exceptions, placing them at the top for immediate attention. It uses **Metric** and **Delta** components to highlight key financial figures and their changes, ensuring clarity and focus. The layout avoids clutter by grouping related metrics and showing only the most critical data, making it easy to scan and act upon.
