This screen is a morning "Market Pulse" dashboard for a portfolio manager. Its primary goal is to instantly surface significant price movements and volatility since yesterday to help them understand market shifts and assess portfolio exposure before trading.

The layout is centered on a **Dashboard** shell. At the top, a **Header** displays the current date, a summary title like "Market Pulse," and quick **Actions** such as "Full Report" or "Alert Settings." Below, a **Section** titled "Key Movers" features a **Comparison** view showing top gainer/loser tickers side-by-side with percentage changes. Beneath that, a **Section** labeled "Portfolio Impact" uses a **Chart** (line or bar) to visualize how yesterday's moves affect the overall book. Finally, a **Section** named "Context & Signals" presents a **DataTable** of relevant news headlines or macro indicators, filtered by a **FilterBar** for quick refinement. The design prioritizes clear, high-level metrics over granular detail, enabling rapid decision-making.
