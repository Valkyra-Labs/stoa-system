The screen is a **dashboard** for the head of school to present the school's performance to the board. It provides an overview of key metrics, trends, and comparisons, allowing the head to showcase the school's achievements and areas for improvement without any interactive changes. 

The layout is divided into **sections** for clarity. A **header** at the top displays the title and a brief summary. Below, a **sidebar** offers quick access to key reports or filters. The **main content area** includes **cards** for key metrics like student performance, staff satisfaction, and budget usage, along with **charts** for visual trends and **tables** for detailed data. A **comparison** section highlights how the school performs relative to its goals or previous terms. The design is clean, static, and ready for presentation.
