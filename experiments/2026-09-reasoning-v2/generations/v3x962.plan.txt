The main goal of this screen is to provide the portfolio manager with a clear, prioritized view of today's market movements and the reasons behind them, enabling rapid decision-making. The layout supports this by placing the most critical alerts and significant changes at the top, ensuring the user sees what matters immediately.

A high-level dashboard section will display key portfolio metrics (e.g., total value, sector shifts) with clear deltas to establish context. Below this, a focused "Significant Moves" section will list the top-impact assets, showing not just *what* changed but *why* (e.g., "Fed Rate Decision" or "Earnings Miss") using concise status tags and direct links to details. This design avoids clutter, uses whitespace to separate concerns, and leverages consistent visual cues for trends and statuses, allowing the manager to scan for anomalies and drill down into specific events with minimal cognitive load.
