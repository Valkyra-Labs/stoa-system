**Main goal**: The screen helps the portfolio manager quickly identify which assets have changed in value since yesterday and understand the reasons behind those changes, enabling informed decision-making.

**Layout support**: The layout prioritizes clarity and focus by placing the most critical information at the top — a summary of key changes and their impact. A timeline or list of significant movements is displayed prominently, with clear indicators of gains or losses. Supporting details, such as reasons for changes, are grouped into sections or cards for easy scanning. Filters and controls are placed in a header or sidebar to allow the manager to refine the data without disrupting the main flow. The design is clean, with enough whitespace to avoid clutter and ensure the manager can process the information efficiently.
