The main goal of the screen is to help a financial adviser compare two investment funds and recommend one to a client. The layout supports this by placing the most important information—fund metrics and key performance indicators—at the top, allowing for quick comparison. The screen features a side-by-side comparison of the funds, with charts and tables that highlight critical data points such as returns, risks, and costs. This structure enables the adviser to easily identify the strengths and weaknesses of each fund, facilitating a well-informed decision. Supporting details, like historical performance and risk profiles, are organized into tabs for deeper analysis when needed.
