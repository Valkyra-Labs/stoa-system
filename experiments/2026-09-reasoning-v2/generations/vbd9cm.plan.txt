The main goal of the screen is to provide the teacher with all the necessary information about a specific student to prepare for a parent-teacher meeting. The layout should be organized to highlight key details about the student's performance, behavior, and any relevant notes or actions the teacher needs to take. The screen will have a header with the student's name and a brief introduction. Below the header, a primary section will display essential metrics such as academic performance, attendance, and behavior. A secondary section will include a timeline of recent events and notes from previous meetings or observations. This layout ensures the teacher can quickly review critical information and prepare effectively for the meeting.
