**Main Goal:** The screen should help the portfolio manager quickly identify what has changed in their portfolio since the previous day and understand the reasons behind those changes, enabling informed decision-making.

**Layout Support:** The layout is structured to prioritize key information. A header with a title and time frame ("Since Yesterday") sets the context. A **Comparison** section shows the portfolio's performance against benchmarks, with **Metrics** highlighting gains/losses. A **Timeline** or **AuditLog** provides a chronological view of changes, while a **DataTable** or **Explorer** allows drilling into specific assets. A **Sidebar** or **Inspector** offers detailed insights into selected items. This structure enables the manager to scan, filter, and explore changes efficiently.
